## Section 2

Section 2 of the principal Act is hereby amended— Amendment

(a) by deleting the fullstop at the end of the definition “financial

year” and substituting for it a semi-colon; and

(b) by adding, after the definition ‘‘financal year”, the following

new definition: — ““Minister” means the Minister for the time being responsible for co-ordination of planning,”’,
