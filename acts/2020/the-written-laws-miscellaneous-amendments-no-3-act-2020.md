THE WRITTEN LAWS (MISCELLANEOUS AMENDMENTS) (NO.3) ACT, 2020

I ASSENT JOHN POMBE JOSEPH MAGUFULI President [15th June, 2020] An Act to amend certain written laws. ENACTED by Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania.

## PART I — PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS

Short title

### Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Written Laws (Miscellaneous Amendments) (No. 3) Act, 2020.

Amendment of certain written laws

### Section 2

The written laws specified in various Parts of this Act are amended in the manner specified in their respective Parts.
