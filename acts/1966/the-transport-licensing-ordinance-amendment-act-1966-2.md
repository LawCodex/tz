# The Transport Licensing Ordinance (Amendment) Act, 1966 (2)

NAKALA MTANDAO (ONLINE DOCUMENT)

THE UNITED REPUBLIC OF TANZANIA'.

No. 36 OF 1966 I ASSENT,

4TH AUGUST, 1966

An Act to amend the Transport Licensing Ordinance 4th AUGUST, 19661 ENACTED by the Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania.

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Transport Licensing Ordinance (Amendment) Act, 1966 and shall be read as one with the Transport Licensing Ordinance.

## Section 2

Section 28A of the Transport Licensing Ordinance is hereby amended by deleting subsection (4) thereof and substituting therefor the following new subsection: ''(4) Where a co-operative society is designated under subsection

(1) the licensing authority shall, unless it is satisfied that the service proposed to be operated by the co-operative society is not necessary or desirable in the public interest, including the interest or interests of persons requiring facilities for transport, grant to the co-operative society a public carrier's licence or a road set vice licence, as the case may be.

(5) Where the licensing authority grants to a co-operative society a licence pursuant to the provisions of subsection (4) such grant shall be in accordance with the matters and conditions specified by the Registrar under subsection (3): Provided that the licensing authority may, where it considers desirable and after consultation with the Registrar, modify any of. such matters or conditions.''

Short title and construction cap.373 Section 28A of the Ordinance amended

NAKALA MTANDAO (ONLINE DOCUMENT)

No. 36

Section 32 of the Ordinance repealed and replaced

## Section 3

Section 32 of the Transport Licensing Ordinance is hereby repealed and replaced by the following new section: ''Fees 32. The Minister may prescribe fees payable in respect of the lodging of any objection under section 27, the grant or variation of licences and in respect of appeals.''
