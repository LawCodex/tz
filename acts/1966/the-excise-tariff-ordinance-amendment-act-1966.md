# The Excise Tariff Ordinance (Amendment) Act, 1966

NAKALA MTANDAO (ONLINE DOCUMENT) THE UNITED REPUBLIC OF TANZANIA rafal No. 45 oF 1966 I ASSENT, ee Hla 4tH AuGuSsT, 1966 An Act to amend the Excise Tariff Ordinance [71TH APRIL, 1966] ENACTED by the Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania. 1, This Act may be cited as the Excise Tariff Ordinance (Amendment) Short title,

\- Act, 1966, shall be read as one with the Excise Tariff Ordinance and shall sonttruction be deemed to have come into operation on the seventh day of April, 1966. commence- Cap. 332

## Section 2

The First Schedule to the Excise Tariff Ordinance is hereby First amended by substituting for the entry in the column headed-Rates of poe to Excise Duty, opposite to item 2 (which relates to sugar) the following amended new entry:— Shs. Cts. “Per hundredweight <~.. a ee A. y 22 40”, and the excise duty is hereby varied accordingly. Passed in the National Assembly on the twenty-first day of July, 1966. Clerk of the National Assembly Printed by the Government Printer, Dar es Salaam.
