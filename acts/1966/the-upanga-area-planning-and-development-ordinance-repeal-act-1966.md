# The Upanga Area (Planning and Development) Ordinance (Repeal) Act, 1966

THE UNITED REPUBLIC OF TANZANIA

No. 38 OF 1966 I ASSENT,

4TH AUGUST, 1966

An Act to repeal the Upanga Area (Planning and Development) Ordinance and to make certain consequential provisions [5TH AUGUST, 1966] ENACTED by the Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania.

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Upanga Area (Planning and Development) Ordinance (Repeal) Act, 1966.

## Section 2

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires, ''principal Ordinance'' means the Upanga Area (Planning and Development) Ordinance.

## Section 3

The principal Ordinance is hereby repealed.
