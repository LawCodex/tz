# The Supplementary Appropriation (1967-68) (No. 2), Act 1967

NAKALA MTANDAO (ONLINE DOCUMENT)

THE UNITED REPUBLIC OF TANZANIA

No. 49 OF 1967 I ASSENT,

27.. NOVEMBER, 1967

An Act to apply a further sum of five million, five hundred thousand shillings out of the Consolidated Fund to the service of the year ending on the thirtieth day of June, one thousand nine hundred and sixty-eight and to appropriate the supply granted 27 NOVEMBER, 19671 ENACTED by the Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania.

## Section 1

-(l) This Act may be cited as the Supplementary Appropriation (1967-68) (No. 2) Act, 1967.

(2) The expressions used in this Act shall have the meanings respectively ascribed thereto in the Exchequer and Audit Ordinance.

## Section 2

The Treasury may issue out of the Consolidated Fund and towards the supply granted for the service of the year ending on the thirtieth day of June, 1968 the sum of five million, five hundred thousand shillings.

## Section 3

The sum granted by section 2 shall be appropriated to, the purposes and in the amounts specified in the second and third columns of the First Schedule to this Act.

## Section 4

In addition to the sums granted out of the Consolidated Fund under section 2, there may be applied out of any moneys directed under section 9 of the Exchequer and Audit Ordinance, to be applied as appropriations-in-aid, the amounts respectively set forth in the fourth column of the First Schedule to this Act.

## Section 5

-(1) The Minister may at any time or times not later than the thirtieth day of June, 1968 borrow within or outside Tanzania, or partly within and partly outside Tanzania, any sum or sums not exceeding in the whole five million, five hundred thousand shillings, by way of loan 1

## Section 6

For the purpose of section 3 of the Development Levy Act, 1965 authority is hereby granted for the development levy credited to the fund established by the said section as at 30th June, 1967 (less a reserve in respect of levy refundable under section 16 of the Act) to an amount not exceeding Shs. 5,500,000 to be paid into the Consolidated Fund and such sum shall be allocated to the purposes and up to a maximum of the amounts specified in the Second Schedule to this Act.
