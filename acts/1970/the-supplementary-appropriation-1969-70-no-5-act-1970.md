# The Supplementary Appropriation (1969-70) (No.5) Act, 1970

NAKALA MTANDAO (ONLINE DOCUMENT)

THE UNITED REPUBLIC OF TANZANIA

No. 17 OF 1970

I ASSENT,

29TH JUNE, 1970

An Act to apply a further sum of forty-six million, three hundred forty-one thousand, four hundred shillings out of the Consolidated Fund to the Service of the year ending on the thirtieth day of June, one thousand nine hundred and seventy and to appropriate the supply granted [29TH JUNE, 1970] ENACTED by the Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania.

## Section 1

-(1) This Act may be cited as the Supplementary Appropriation (I 969 / 70) (No. 5) Act, 1970 and shall be read as one with the Appropriation Act, 1969.

(2) The expressions used in this Act shall have the meanings respectively ascribed thereto in the Exchequer and Audit Ordinance.

## Section 2

The Treasury may issue out of the Consolidated Fund and apply towards the supply granted for the service of the year ending on the thirtieth day of June, 1970, the sum of forty-six million, three hundred forty-one thousand, four hundred shillings.

## Section 3

The sum granted by section 2 shall be appropriated to the purposes and in the amounts specified in the second and fifth columns of the tion of Schedule to this Act.

## Section 4

(1) The Minister may at any time or times not later than the thirtieth day of June, 1970, borrow within or outside Tanzania or partly within and partly outside Tanzania any such or sums not exceeding in the whole forty-six million, three hundred forty-one thousand, four hundred shillings by way of loan, advance, the issue of bills or bank overdraft and on such terms and conditions as the Minister may deem expedient, and may charge any such loan or advance on any of the assets of the United Republic (including securities forming part of the Consolidated Fund). 1
