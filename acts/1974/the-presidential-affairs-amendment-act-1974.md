# The Presidential Affairs (Amendment) Act, 1974

NAKALA MTANDAO (ONLINE DOCUMENT) THE UNITED REPUBLIC OF TANZANIA | No, 25 oF 1974 I ASSENT, ; Oe) President ZO TH rucust, 1974 An Act to amend the Presidential Affairs Act, 1962

ENACTED by the Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania:

## Section 1

This Act may becited as the Presidential Affairs (Amendment) Short title Act, 1974 and shall be read as one with the Presidential Affairs Act, ad construct: 1962, Cap. 502

## Section 2

Section 10 of the Presidential Affairs Act, 1962 is amended by Section 10 deleting the words “three million shillings” which occur.in’ the third 9°” line thereof and substituting therefor the words “five million shillings”. amended

Passed in the National Assembly.on the twenty-fourth day of July,

1974\. | eae Monn hake. Acting Clerk of the National Assembly Printed by the Government Printer, Dar es Salaam, Tanzania.
