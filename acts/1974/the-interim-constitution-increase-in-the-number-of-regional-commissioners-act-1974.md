# The Interim Constitution (Increase in the Number of Regional Commissioners) Act, 1974

NAKALA MTANDAO (ONLINE DOCUMENT) THE UNITED REPUBLIC-OP, TANZANIA. No. 3 oF 1974 T ASSENT, Tesident — ALE. Apri, 1974 An Act to provide for an increase in the Number of Regional Commissioners who shall be Members of the National Assembly [Ist JANUARY, 1974]

ENACTED by the Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania.

WHEREAS subsection (1) of section 24 of the Interim Constitution of Tanzania, 1965, provides that seventeen Regional Commissioners appointed for the Regions in Tanganyika shall be’ Members of the National Assembly :

AND WHEREAS the President-has increased the number of Regions in Tanganyika from eighteen to twenty:

AND WHEREAS subsection (3),of the said.section 24 of the Interim Constitution provides that if the number of Regions is other than seventeen in Tanganyika, an Act of Parliament may provide for a corresponding increase or decrease in the number of Regional Commissioners who shall be Members of the National Assembly:

AND WHEREAS by the Interim Constitution (Increase in the Number of Regional Commissioners) Act, 1971 the number of Regional Com- Acts, 1971 missioners who shall be Members of Parliament was increased from ~~ seventeen to eighteen :
