## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Government Proceedings (Amendment) Act, 1974 and shall be read as one with the Government Proceedings Act, 1967.

## Section 3

Section 10 of the Government Proceedings Act, 1967 is repealed and replaced by the following section: ''Transfer of proceedings where set off or counter claims made

1974

Government Proceedings (Amendment)

10\. If in any case proceedings have been instituted by the Government in a magistrate's court and the defendant satisties the court that(a) he has a claim against the Government;
