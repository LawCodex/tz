## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the “Industrial Shares (Acquisition) sport tite (Amendment) Act, 1974 and shall be read as one with the Industrial Acts 1967 Shares (Acquisition) Act, 1967. No. 5

## Section 2

Section 3 of the Industrial Shares (Acquisition) Act, 1967 is amended . by deleting subsection (14) .and substituting therefor the following subsection : —

“(1a) Notwithstanding the provisions of subsection (1) orders under that subsection may be made in respect of the companies specified in the Schedule to this Act as Tanzania Bata Shoe Company Limited (which company is now called Tanzania Shoe Company Limited), Tanganyika Portland Cement Company Limited and Tanganyika Extract Company Limited vesting in the Treasury Registrar all the shares in those companies.”’.
