THE PUBLIC PROCUREMENT (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2016 I ASSENT, JOHN POMBE JOSEPH MAGUFULI President [ 7 th July, 2016 ]

An Act to amend the Public Procurement Act with a view to enabling efficiency in regulating procurement processes, to ensure value for money in public procurement and to provide for other related matters. ENACTED by Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania

## PART I — PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS

Construction Cap. 410

### Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Public Procurement (Amendment) Act, 2016 and shall be read as one with the Public Procurement Act, hereinafter referred to as the “principal Act”.
