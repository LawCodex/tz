# The Supplementary Appropriation (No.2 for the Financial year 197576) (No.2) Act, 1976

NAKALA MTANDAO (ONLINE DOCUMENT)

THE UNITED REPUBLIC OF TANZANIA

No. 6 OF 1976 I ASSENT,

10......JULY, TH 1976

An Act to apply a further sum of one hundred seventeen million, one hundred seventy-five thousand, four hundred fifty-seven shillings out of the Consolidated Fund to the Service of the year ending on the thirtieth day of June, one thousand nine hundred and seventy-six and to appropriate the supply granted [ JULY, 1976] ENACTED by the Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania.

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the Supplementary Appropriation (No. 2 for Financial Year 1975/76) (No. 2) Act, 1976 and shall be read and as one with the Appropriation Act, 1975.

Short title and enterpretation

(2) The expressions used in this Act shall have the meanings respecti- Cap. 439 vely ascribed thereto in the Exchequer and Audit Ordinance.

## Section 2

The Treasury may issue out of the Consolidated Fund and apply towards the supply granted for the Service of the year ending on the thirtieth day of June, 1976 the sum of one hundred seventeen million, one hundred seventy-five thousand, four hundred fifty-seven shillings.

## Section 3

The sum granted by section 2 shall be appropriated to the purposes and in the amounts specified in the second and fifth columns of the tion of Schedule to this Act. moneys voted

## Section 4

-(l) The Minister may at any time or times not later than the Power of the Minister to thirtieth day of June, 1976 borrow within or outside Tanzania or partly borrow within and partly outside Tanzania any sum or sums not exceeding in the whole one hundred seventeen million, one hundred seventy-five thousand, four hundred fifty-seven shillings by way of loan, advance, the issue of bills as bank overdraft and on such terms and conditions as the Minister may deem expedient, and may charge any such loan or advance of any of the assets of the United Republic (including securities forming part of Consolidated Fund).
