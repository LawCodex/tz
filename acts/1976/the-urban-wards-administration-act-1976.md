# The Urban Wards (Administration) Act, 1976

THE URBAN WARDS (ADMINISTRATION) ACT, 1976 I ASSENT,

31ST AUGUST, 1976

An Act to make provision for the establishment in urban areas of Ward Assemblies and Ward Councils, to provide for the functions of such Assemblies and Councils and for other matters incidental thereto or connected therewith [ ] ENACTED by the Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania.

## PART I — PRELIMINARY

### Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Urban Wards (Administration) Act, 1976 and shall come into operation on such date as the Minister may, by notice in the Gazette, appoint.

"ward'' means a division of a district declared or deemed to have been declared to be a ward of such district under the provisions of section 4 of the District Development Councils (Elections) Act, 1975; ''Ward Assembly'' means the Ward Assembly of a ward established under the provisions of section 4; ''Ward Council'' means the Ward Council of a ward established under the provisions of section 4.

(2) For the purposes of this Act every ward within a district declared under the provisions of subsection (1) of section 3 to be a ward to which the provisions of this Act apply shall be deemed to be a ward in an urban area.

### Section 3

-(i) The Minister may, by order published in the Gazette, declare that the ward specified in the order shall, with effect from such date as may be specified in the order (hereinafter referred to as ''the effective date''), be a ward to which the provisions of this Act apply.

(2) Where an order under subsection (1) is made m respect of a ward the Minister may, in the same order or in any subsequent order published in the Gazette, publish a list of all such wards within the district describing the boundaries thereof and assigning names thereto and such wards shall be known accordingly, whether or not such description of boundaries or names is the same as that used In a declaration made or deemed to have been made under the provisions of section 4 of the District Development Councils (Elections) Act, 1975.

(3) If any question arises whether any place is or is not within any ward, and such question does not appear to be determined by any order referred to in subsection (2), or other evidence, it shall be referred to the Minister, and a certificate under his hand shall be conclusive on the question, and judicial notice shall be taken thereof.

## PART II — WARD ASSEMBLY AND WARD COUNCIL

Establishment of Ward Assembly and Ward Council Ward Assembly

### Section 4

Where an order under subsection (1) of section 3 is made in respect of a ward there shall be established, in respect of every such ward, a Ward Assembly and a Ward Council.

### Section 5

-(1) Every Ward Assembly shall consist of(a) members elected to the Assembly in accordance with the provisions of this section; and

(b) members of the Ward Council elected to the Council pursuant to the provisions of subsection (1) of section 6, who shall be members of the Assembly by virtue of their membership of the Council.

(2) As soon as may be practicable after the effective date, the Area Commissioner shall, with the consent of the Minister, appoint a date for the election of Ward Assemblies in the district.

(3) No person shall be qualified for election as a member of a Ward Assembly unless he is ordinarily resident in the ward and has attained the apparent age of eighteen years.

(4) On the date appointed under subsection (2) there shall be elected, for every Ward Assembly, as many members as there are Cells in the ward within the jurisdiction of the Ward Assembly, that is to say, one member from each Cell, and such members shall assume their offices with effect from the date on which they are elected.

(5) Every elected member of a Ward Assembly shall, unless he sooner ceases to be a member for any reason whatsoever. hold office until such time as new members of the Ward Assembly are elected in accordance with the provisions of subsection (7), and at the expiration of his term of office shall be eligible for re-election

(6) Any casual vacancy occurring in the office of an elected member of a Ward Assembly may be filled by the election of a new member: Provided that(a) no person shall be elected under this subsection unless he is a person who would qualify for election as a member under subsection (3);

(b) the new, member shall be elected in the same Cell and in the same manner in which the former member was elected;

(c) any person elected under this subsection shall hold office for the reminder of the term of office of his predecessor.

(7) The Area Commissioner shall, within thirty days after the expiry of twelve months from the date on which a Ward Assembly was elected, cause election to be held for the election of new members of the Assembly, and all members so elected shall assume their offices with effect from the date on which they are elected.

### Section 6

-(1) As soon as may be practicable after its election every Ward Assembly shall meet for the purpose of electing a Ward Council for the ward. (,2) The meeting convened pursuant to the provision of subsection (1) shall be presided over by the Chairman of the Party referred to in the proviso to subsection (1) of section 9 or, in his absence or inability to act for any reason whatsoever, a chairman elected by the Ward Assembly from amongst its members.

(3) All other meetings of a Ward Assembly shall be presided over by the Chairman of the Ward Council referred to in subsection (1) of section 9 or, in his absence or inability to act for any reason whatsoever, a temporary chairman elected by the Ward Council from amongst its members.

(4) It shall be lawful for a Ward Assembly, at a meeting convened pursuant to the provisions of subsection (1), to enlarge or reduce, subject to the provisions of subsection (1) of section, 7, the membership of the Ward Council to be elected.

(5) In addition to the meeting convened pursuant to the provisions of subsection (1) every Ward Assembly shall meet not less than twice every year and may meet at such additional times as may be necessary or expedient for the proper performance of its functions.

Casual vacancies in membership of Ward Council

### Section 8

Any casual vacancy occurring in the membership of a Ward Council may be filled by the Ward Council by appointment of a member from amongst the residents of the ward: Provided that(a) no person shall be appointed under this section unless he is a person who would qualify for election as a member under subsection (2) of section 7;

(b) any person appointed under this section shall hold office for the remainder of the term of office of his predecessor;

(c) the Ward Council may, if in its opinion it is desirable so to do, convene an extraordinary meeting of the Ward Assembly for election of a member to fill the vacancy.

(2) A Ward Council may, by a majority of not less than two-thirds of the total number of members present and voting, remove from office any office bearer of the Council elected by the Council in accordance with the provisions of subsection (1) and elect another member to that office.

### Section 10

Every Ward Council shall be a body corporate with perpetual succession and a common seal -and shall be capable in law of suing and being sued in its corporate name, of purchasing, holding, alienating, managing and disposing of any property whatsoever, whether movable or immovable, and whether by way of investment or otherwise, and of entering into any such contract as may be necessary or expedient for the performance of its functions under this Act or any other written law.

### Section 11

-(1) Every Ward Assembly and Ward Council shall have such functions as are conferred upon them by this Act or any Other Written law, and in addition thereto the functions of a Ward Council Shall be-

(a) to do all such acts and things as are necessary or expedient for the economic and social development of the ward;

(b) to initiate and undertake any task, venture or enterprise as is designed to ensure the welfare and well being of the residents of the ward;

(c) to plan and co-ordinate the activities of and render assistance and advice to the residents of the ward engaged in any activity or industry whatsoever;

(d) to encourage the residents of the ward in undertaking and participating in Ujamaa activities;

(e) to participate. by way of partnership or otherwise. in economic enterprises with other Ward Councils;

(f) to promote the establishment and development of co-operative enterprises and activities within the ward;

(g) to regulate and exercise control over economic activities undertaken within the ward.

(2) A Ward Council shall have the power to do all such acts and things as appear to it to be necessary or convenient for or m connection with the carrying out of its functions or incidental or conducive to their proper performance.

(3) A Ward Council may establish committees and delegate to such committees any of its functions.

## PART III — MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS

### Section 15

-(1) Where the President is satisfied that for the proper and efficient administration or management of the affairs of a ward. or of the conduct of the affairs of its residents, or for the political, economic or social development of a ward, it Is necessary or desirable that the Ward Council of the ward enjoy, in relation to the ward or its residents, any power (including judicial, quasi judicial or administrative power) which Is by any other written law conferred upon any authority or person, whether generally or in relation to any specified area (whether or not such specified area includes the ward) or in relation to any group of persons or specified matter, the President May, by order in the Gazette, provide that, subject to such limitations and restrictions as he, may in such order specify, the Ward Council or any organ, or committee of the Ward Council may exercise such power in relation to the ward or its residents as if such power, subject to such limitations and restrictions. if any, specified in the order, were, in relation to the ward or its residents, conferred upon the Ward Council or such organ or committee of the Ward Council as may be specified in the order, by such other written law.

(2) Where the President makes an order under subsection (1) conferring upon a Ward Council or upon any organ or committee of a Ward Council any power provided for in any other written law, he may, by the same or any subsequent order, modify such other written law to such extent as he may deem necessary for the avoidance Of any inconsistency or conflict between the provisions of such other written law and the provisions of the Order made under subsection (1), or for providing for an appeal against the decision of the Ward Council in the exercise of such power or for any matter incidental to or connected with the exercise by the Ward Council or organ or committee of the Ward Council of such power.
