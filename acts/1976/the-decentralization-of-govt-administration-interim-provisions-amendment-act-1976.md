# The Decentralization of Govt. Administration (Interim Provisions) (Amendment) Act, 1976

NAKALA MTANDAO (ONLINE DOCUMENT)

THE UNITED REPUBLIC OF TANZANIA

No. 15 OF 1976

I ASSENT,

12TH NOVEMBER, 1976

An Act to amend the Decentralization of Government Administration (Interim Provisions) Act, 1972 to provide for the representation of Villages, Ujamaa Villages and Urban Wards on District Development Councils [ ] ENACTED by the Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania.

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Decentralization of Government Administration (Interim Provisions) (Amendment) Act, 1976 and shall be read as one with the Decentralization of Government Administration (Interim Provisions) Act. 1972 (hereinafter referred to as ''the principal Act'').

Short title

## Section 2

Section 7 of the principal Act is amended in subsection (3) by deleting paragraphs (c), (d), (e) and (f) thereof and substituting therefor the following paragraphs: -

Section 7 amended

"(c) the District Development Director for the district;

(d) the constituency member of the National Assembly representing the district and all other members of the National Assembly ordinarily resident within the district;

(e) all persons elected one each by the Village Assemblies of all the villages within the district which are registered under section 4 of the Villages and Ujamaa Villages (Registration. Designation and Administration) Act, 1975;

(f) all persons elected one each by Ward Assemblies of all wards established within the district under section 4 of the Urban Wards (Administration) Act, 1976;

(g) all the members of the District Planning and Implementation Committee elected to that Committee by the District Development Council,''
