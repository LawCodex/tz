# The Finance Act, 1976

THE FINANCE ACT, 1976

I ASSENT,

31st ......JULY, 1976

An Act to impose and alter certain Taxes and Duties and to amend certain Written Laws relating to Taxes and Duties and for purposes connected therewith and incidental thereto [

]

ENACTED by the Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania.

### Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Finance Act, 1976.

Short title

## PART I — AMENDMENTS To THE CUSTOMS TARIFF ACT, 1969

### Section 2

--(l) This Part shall be read as one with the Customs Tariff Act, 1969 and shall be deemed to have come into operation on 18th June, 1976.

(2) The passage ''(same)'' appearing in the amendments made by this Part to the First Schedule and the Second Schedule to the Customs Tariff Act, 1969 means that, except as specifically amended by this Part, the tariff heading or the import duty or, as the case may be, the suspended duty (according to the column m which the tariff number in relation to which such passage appears) shall continue the same as it was immediately prior to the coming into operation of this Part.
