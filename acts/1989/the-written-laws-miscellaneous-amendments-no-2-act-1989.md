## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Written Laws.(Miscellaneous Amendments) (No. 2) Act, 1989.

## Section 2

The Written Laws specified in the first and second columns of the Schedule to this Act are hereby amended each in the manner specified opposite to it in the third column of that Schedule.
