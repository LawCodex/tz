# The Appropriation Act, 1971

THE UNITED REPUBLIC OF TANZANIA

No. 21 OF 1971

I ASSENT,

4TH AUGUST, 1971

An Act to apply a sum of two thousand four hundred and fifty-one million five hundred and six thousand four hundred shillings out of the Consolidated Fund to the Service of the year ending on the thirtieth day of June, one thousand nine hundred and seventytwo, to appropriate the supply granted for such year, to authorize the reallocation of certain appropriations and to provide for matters incidental thereto and connected therewith [6TH AUGUST, 1971] ENACTED by the Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania.

## Section 1

-(1) This Act may be cited as the Appropriation Act, 1971.

(2) The expressions used in this Act shall have the meanings respectively ascribed thereto m the Exchequer and Audit Ordinance. ... ..

## Section 2

The Treasury may issue out of the Consolidated Fund and apply towards the supply granted for the service of the year ending on the thirtieth day of June, 1972, the sum of two thousand four hundred and fifty-one million, five hundred and six thousand four hundred shillings.

## Section 4

The Minister may, by certificate under his hand, raise the sums of moneys appropriated by the First Schedule hereto for any of the purposes specified m the second colum of the Second Schedule hereto by such further sum or sums of money not exceeding in aggregate the amounts specified in the third and fourth columns of the said Second Schedule in relation to such purpose, and where such a certificate is issued the amount specified therein shall be a charge on and be paid out of the Consolidated Fund, and the provisions of sections 2 and 3 shall take effect as if the total sum granted out of the Consolidated Fund and the amounts appropriated for the purpose specified in such certificate were raised by the amount or amounts specified in the certificate.

Agency account

## Section 5

Notwithstanding the provisions of section 20 of the Exchequer and Audit Ordinance, after the close of the financial year ending on the thirtieth day of June, 1972 it shall be lawful to defray expenditure from the unexpended balance of any moneys withdrawn from the Consolidated Fund to the extent that such expenditure is authorized by Financial Orders relating to Agency Accounts issued from time to time by the Minister for Finance.

Powers of Minister to borrow

## Section 6

-(1) The Minister may, at any time or times not later than the thirtieth day of June, 1972, borrow within or outside the United Republic or partly within and partly outside the United Republic any sum or sums not exceeding m the whole the total sum granted or deemed to have been granted out of the Consolidated Fund by virtue of the provisions of section 2 by way of loan, advance, the issue of bills or bank overdraft and on such terms and conditions as the Minister may deem expedient, and may charge any such loan or advance on any of the assets of the United Republic (including securities forming part of the Consolidated Fund).

(2) Any money borrowed under this section shall be placed to the credit of the exchequer account and shall form part of the Consolidated Fund, and be available m any manner m which such Fund is available.

(3) Any money borrowed under this section shall be subject to repayment not later than the thirty-firstday of October, 1972 and any sum required for the repayment of any money so borrowed, and of any interest payable thereon shall be charged on and paid out of the Consolidated Fund.

(4) The powers conferred upon the Minister by this section shall be in addition to the powers of the Minister under the Local Loans Ordinance and the Loans and Guarantees Act, 1962.
