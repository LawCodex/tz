## Section 1

-(l) This Act may be cited as the Supplementary Appropriation (1970/71 (No. 3)) (No. 2) Act, 1971 and shall be read as one with the Appropriation Act, 1970.

(2) The expressions used in this Act shall have the meanings respectively ascribed thereto in the Exchequer and Audit Ordinance.
