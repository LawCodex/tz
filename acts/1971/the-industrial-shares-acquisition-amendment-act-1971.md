ENACTED» by the Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania.

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Industrial Shares (Acquisition) Short title (Amendment) Act, 1971-and shall be read as one with the Industrial 4%2967 Shares (Acquisition) Act, 1967.

## Section 2

Section 3, of the Industrial Shares (Acquisition) Act, 1967 is Section 3 amended by adding immediately below subsection (1), the following amended subsection :—

“(1A) Notwithstanding the provisions’ef subsection (1) an order under that subsection-may be made in respect of the Tanzania Bata Shoe Company Limited vesting in the Treasury Registrar all the shares in that company.”’.
