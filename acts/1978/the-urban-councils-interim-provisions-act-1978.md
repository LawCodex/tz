No. 11 OF 1978 I ASSENT

## PART I — PRELIMINARY

### Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Urban Councils (Interim Provisions) Act, 1978 and shall come into operation, on the first day of July, 1978.

### Section 2

This Act shall, unless sooner repealed expire on such date as the President may by order in the Gazette prescribe.
