## Section 7

The principal Act is hereby amended by adding, immediately after section 69 the following new sections: "Establishment of Wildlife Protection Fund

69.-(1) There is hereby established a fund which shall be known as the Wildlife Protection Fund.

(2) The Wildlife Protection Fund shall consist of:...

(a) such, sums as may be provide. for the purpose by Parliament;

(b) twenty-five per cent of the proceeds of the sale of every animal, trophy, weapon, vehicle, vessel, aircraft, tent or other article which 'is forfeited pursuant to section 78 and sold or disposed of in any other way for money;

(c) any sum or property Which may many manner become payable, into the Fund;

(d) any sum granted to the Fund by the Government or any person.

(3) The Minister shall make rules regulating the functions, and use of the Fund, and shall, after consultation with the Minister for the time being responsible for finance, make rules regulating the operations of the Fund.
