# The Supplementary Appropriation (No.3 for 197778) Act, 1978

NAKALA MTANDAO (ONLINE DOCUMENT)

THE UNITED REPUBLIC OF TANZANIA

No. 8 OF 1978 I ASSENT

19TH MAY, 1978

An Act to apply a further sum of seventy-three million, two hundred thirty-six thousand, eight hundred and forty shillings out of the Consolidated Fund to the Service of the year ending on the thirtieth day of June, one thousand nine hundred and seventy-eight and to appropriate the supply granted ] [ ENACTED by the Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania.

## Section 1

-(1) This Act may be cited as the Supplementary Appropriation (No. 3 for Financial year 1977/781) (No, 4) Act, 1978 and shall be read as one with The Appropriation Act, 1977.

Short title and interpretation

(2) The expressions used in this Act shall have the meanings respectively ascribed thereto in the Exchequer and Audit Ordinance.

Cap. 439

## Section 2

The Treasury may issue out of the Consolidated Fund and apply towards the supply granted for the Service of the year ending on the thirtieth day of June, 1978 the sum of seven-three million, two hundred thirty-six -thousand. eight hundred and forty shillings

Issue of Shs. 73,236,840 out of the Consolidated Fund

## Section 3

The Sum granted by section 2 shall be appropriated to the and in the amounts specified in the second and fifth columns of the Schedule to this Act.

Approprition of money's voted
