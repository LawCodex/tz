### Section 5

-(I) The Valuation Surveyor or any person assisting him may(a) for the purpose of preparing or checking an entry in a roll or supple, mentary roll, or for the purpose of preparing or checking any rate, enter into or upon any rateable property at any reasonable hour in the day time and survey or inspect such property;

(2) Any person who:(a) unreasonably refuses access to the rateable property in contravention of the provisions of paragraph (a) of subsection (1);
