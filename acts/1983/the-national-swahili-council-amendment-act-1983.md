## Section 1

This Act may, be cited as the National Swahili Council (Amendment) Act, 1983, and shall be read as one with the National Swahili Council Act 1967, in this Act referred as "the principal Act''..

## Section 2

The principal Act is hereby amended(a), m section 1, by deleting the word "Swahili " and substituting for it the word ''Kiswahili'';.

(b) by deleting the word "Swahili" wherever it occurs in the Act and substituting for it the word ''Kiswahili''.
