THE WRITTEN LAWS (MISCELLANEOUS AMENDMENTS) (NO. 3) ACT, 2023 I ASSENT SAMIA SULUHU HASSAN, President 29th September, 2023

An Act to amend various written laws. ENACTED by the Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania.

## PART I — PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS

Short title

### Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Written Laws (Miscellaneous Amendments) (No. 3) Act, 2023.

Amendments of certain written laws

### Section 2

The written laws specified in various Parts of this Act are amended in the manner specified in their respective Parts.

## PART II

AMENDMENT OF THE EXPORT PROCESSING ZONES ACT, (CAP. 373) Construction Cap. 373

### Section 3

This Part shall be read as one with the Export Processing Zones Act, hereinafter referred to as the “principal Act”.

Amendment of section 2

### Section 4

The principal Act is amended in section 2 by deleting the definition of the term “Minister” and

substituting for it the following: ““Minister” means the Minister responsible for export processing zones;”. Amendment of section 14

### Section 5

The principal Act is amended in section 14(2) by deleting the words “responsible for industries” appearing in paragraph (a).

## PART III

AMENDMENT OF THE SPECIAL ECONOMIC ZONES ACT, (CAP. 420) Construction Cap. 420

### Section 6

This Part shall be read as one with the Special Economic Zones Act, hereinafter referred to as the “principal Act”.

Amendment of section 3

### Section 7

The principal Act is amended in section 3 by deleting the definition of the term “Minister” and substituting for it the following: ““Minister” means the Minister responsible for special economic zones;”. Passed by the National Assembly on the 6th September, 2023
