THE WRITTEN LAWS (MISCELLANEOUS AMENDMENTS) ACT, 2023 I ASSENT SAMIA SULUHU HASSAN President [5th July, 2023] An Act to amend certain laws. ENACTED by the Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania.

## PART I — PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS

Short title

### Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Written Laws (Miscellaneous Amendments) Act, 2023.

Amendment of certain laws

### Section 2

The written laws specified in various Parts of this Act are amended in the manner specified in their respective Parts.
