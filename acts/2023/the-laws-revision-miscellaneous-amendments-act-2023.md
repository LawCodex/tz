# The Laws Revision (Miscellaneous Amendments) Act, 2023

ISSN 0856 - 034X THE UNITED REPUBLIC OF TANZANIA 14th July, 2023

No. 5

ACT SUPPLEMENT To the Gazette of the United Republic of Tanzania No.28 Vol. 104 dated 14th July, 2023 Printed by the Government Printer, Dodoma by Order of Government

THE LAWS REVISION (MISCELLANEOUS AMENDMENTS) ACT, 2023 I ASSENT SAMIA SULUHU HASSAN President [5th July, 2023]

An Act to amend certain laws with a view to address various shortcomings realised in those laws in the course of laws revision exercise. ENACTED by the Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania.

## PART I — PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS

Short Title

### Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Laws Revision (Miscellaneous Amendments) Act, 2023.

Amendment of certain written laws

### Section 2

The written laws specified in various Parts of this Act are amended in the manner specified in their respective Parts.

## PART II — AMENDMENT OF THE CORPORATIONS SOLE (ESTABLISHMENT) ACT, (CAP. 119)

Construction Cap. 119

### Section 3

This Part shall be read as one with the Corporations Sole (Establishment) Act, hereinafter referred to as the “principal Act”.

Amendment of section 5

Cap. 134

2023

### Section 4

The principal Act is amended in section 5(2) by deleting the words “the National Bank of Commerce, the Tanzania Investment Bank, Cooperative and Rural Development Bank, save in accordance with the regulations made under this Act” and substituting for them the words “or any financial institution subject to the provisions of the Government Loans, Guarantees and Grants Act,”.

## PART III — AMENDMENT OF THE EXCISE (MANAGEMENT AND TARIFF) ACT, (CAP. 147)

Construction Cap. 147

### Section 5

This Part shall be read as one with the Excise (Management and Tariff) Act, hereinafter referred to as the “principal Act”.

General amendment

### Section 6

The principal Act is amended generally, save for section 1, by deleting the word “Excise” wherever it appears in the Act and substituting for it the word “Authority”.

Amendment of section 2

### Section 7

The principal Act is amended in section 2, by(a) deleting the definition of the term ““Excise” or “the Excise””; and

(b) adding in the appropriate alphabetical order the following new definition““Authority” means the Tanzania Revenue Authority established Cap. 399 under the Tanzania Revenue Authority Act;”.

Amendment of section 100

### Section 8

The principal Act is amended in section 100 by deleting the words “Excise revenue” appearing in paragraph

(a) and substituting for them the words “Consolidated Fund”.

Repeal of section 107

### Section 9

The principal Act is amended by repealing section 107.

## PART IV

AMENDMENT OF THE INCOME TAX ACT, (CAP. 332) Construction Cap. 332

Amendment of section 65E

### Section 10

This Part shall be read as one with the Income Tax Act, hereinafter referred to as the “principal Act”.

### Section 11

The principal Act is amended in section 65E(2), by(a) adding immediately after paragraph (a) the following: “(b) for royalty incurred by a person Cap. 123 under the Mining Act;”; and

(b) renaming paragraphs (b), (c) and (d) as paragraphs (c), (d) and (e), respectively.

## PART V

AMENDMENT OF THE INTERPRETATION OF LAWS ACT, (CAP. 1) Construction Cap. 1

### Section 12

This Part shall be read as one with the Interpretation of Laws Act, hereinafter referred as the “principal Act”.

Addition of section 49A

### Section 13

The principal Act is amended by adding immediately after section 49 the following: “Power to 49A. Where a written law amend and confers powers to the Minister to alter amend, vary or replace provisions Schedules of the Schedule concerning the conduct of a governing body, such powers shall, unless the provisions are substantively provided in the Act, not include powers to amend, vary or replace provisions relating to(a) appointment and termination of a member of the governing body; or

(b) determination of composition and tenure of office of members of the governing body.”. Amendment of section 84

### Section 14

The principal Act is amended in section 84(a) in subsection (1), by adding the words “of enactment” immediately after the word “language”;

(b) in subsection (2), by deleting the words “are currently” and substituting for them the words “were enacted”;

(c) by deleting subsection (3) and substituting for it the following: “(3) Without prejudice to subsection (1), a law enacted in Kiswahili shall, within ninety days after the date of commencement of such law, be translated into English language.”; and

(d) by deleting subsection (6).

## PART VI

AMENDMENT OF THE LAWS REVISION ACT, (CAP. 4)

Construction Cap. 4

### Section 15

This Part shall be read as one with the Laws Revision Act, hereinafter referred to as the “principal Act”.

Amendment of section 7

### Section 16

The principal Act is amended in section 7, by(a) designating the content of section 7 as subsection (1); and

(b) adding immediately after subsection (1) as designated the following: “(2) The Chief Parliamentary Draftsman may omit any schedule of an Act, if in his opinion its inclusion is inconvenient in the preparation and publication of the Revised Edition: Provided that, the omitted schedule shall continue to form part of the Act.”;

Amendment of section 8

2023

### Section 17

The principal Act is amended in section 8(a) by adding imediately after subsection (3) the following: “(4) The exercise of powers under this section shall not affect anything, act, legal proceeding or subsidiary legislation, done, instituted, or as the case may be, made before the coming into operation of the Revised Edition.”;

(b) by renumbering subsections (4) and (5) as subsections (5) and (6), respectively; and

(c) in subsection (6) as renumbered by deleting reference to “subsection (4)” and substituting for it reference to “subsection (5)”.
