THE SUPPLEMENTARY APPROPRIATION (FOR FINANCE YEAR 2024/2025) ACT, 2025 I ASSENT SAMIA SULUHU HASSAN, President [4th March, 2025]

An Act to apply a sum of nine hundred forty-five billion, seven hundred twenty million, five hundred seventy-one thousand, six hundred sixty-two shillings and seventy-nine cents out of the Consolidated Fund to the Service of the year ending the thirtieth day of June, 2025, to appropriate the supply granted. ENACTED by Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania. Short title Act No. 5 of 2024

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Supplementary Appropriation (for Financial Year 2024/25) Act, 2025 and shall be read together with Appropriation Act, 2024.

Interpretation Cap. 348 Cap. 439

## Section 2

The expressions used in this Act shall each have the meaning ascribed to them in the Public Finance Act and the Budget Act.

Issuance of Shs. 945,720,571,662.79 out of Consolidated Fund

## Section 3

The Treasury may issue out of the Consolidated Fund and apply towards the supply granted for the service of the year ending on the thirtieth

Appropriation of moneys voted

day of June, 2025, the sum of nine hundred forty-five billion, seven hundred twenty million, five hundred seventy-one thousand, six hundred sixty-two shillings and seventy-nine cents.

## Section 4

The sum granted under section 3 shall be appropriated for the purposes and in the amounts specified in the third, fourth and fifth columns respectively of the Schedule to this Act.
