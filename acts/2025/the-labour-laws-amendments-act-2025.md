# The Labour Laws (Amendments) Act, 2025

The Labour Laws (Amendments) Act, ISSN 0856 - 035X THE UNITED REPUBLIC OF TANZANIA 14th March, 2025

No. 4

ACT SUPPLEMENT To The Special Gazette Of The United Republic Of Tanzania No. 4 Vol. 106 Dated 14th March, 2025

Printed By The Government Printer, Dodoma By Order Of Government

THE LABOUR LAWS (AMENDMENTS) ACT, 2025 I ASSENT SAMIA SULUHU HASSAN, President [4th March, 2025]

An Act to amend various Labour laws. ENACTED by the Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania.

## PART I — PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS

Short title

### Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Labour Laws (Amendments) Act, 2025.

Amendment of labour laws

### Section 2

The labour laws specified in various Parts of this Act are amended in the manner specified in their respective Parts.
