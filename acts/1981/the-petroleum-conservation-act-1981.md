# The Petroleum (Conservation) Act, 1981

THE PETROLEUM (CONSERVATION) ACT, 1981 An Act to provide for the regulation of the import, transport, storage, distribution, sale and use of petroleum and petroleum products, and connected matters [ ] ENACTED by the Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania.

## PART I — PRELIMINARY

### Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Petroleum (Conservation) Act, 1981. and shall come into operation on such date as the Minister may, by notice in the Gazette appoint, which, in this Act, is referred to as the "appointed day".

### Section 2

The provisions of this Act shall apply to all persons, all departments of the Government, all public corporations and all companies established under the Companies Ordinance and to the Tanzania Peoples's Defence Forces and all persons employed by or under them, and to all such other entities or persons who may deal in petroleum or petroleum products: Provided that any regulations made under this Act may be expressed not to so apply.

### Section 4

No petroleum shall be imported, unloaded, landed, loaded, transhipped, transported, stored or sold to customers save in accordance with the provisions of this Act.

### Section 11

The owner or master of every ship -carrying a cargo any part of which consist of petroleum who. upon entering a port fads to give such notice to the port authorities as may be required by regulations made under this Act shall be guilty of an offence and shall upon conviction be liable to a fine not exceeding twenty thousand shillings.

### Section 12

Where a person contravenes a provision of any of the Regulations made under this Act for which no specific penalty is provided he shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding twenty thousand shillings or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding three years or to both that fine and imprisonment.

### Section 14

-(l) Where any lawful act or thing required or permitted to be done by or under this Act was done before the commencement of this Act, it shall be deemed to have been done under or by virtue of this Act.

(2) For the avoidance of doubt no otherwise lawful act or thing as is referred to in subsection (1) shall be invalid or otherwise unlawful on the grounds only that it was done before the enactment of this Act.

### Section 15

Notwithstanding the repeal of the Petroleum Ordinance(a) all subsidiary legislation and all exemptions made or given under that ordinance which is in force on the appointed day shall be deemed to have been made or given under this Act, and shall remain in force until revoked by subsidiary legislation made under this Act or until it otherwise expires or ceases to have effect;

(b) all officers appointed under the Petroleum Ordinance to perform functions specified under the rules made under that Ordinance, shall, unless their appointments are revoked or they otherwise cease to hold office or are re-appointed, be deemed to be authorized officers appointed under this Act.

Validation

### Section 16

The Minister may, by notice in the Gazette, at any time before the expiry of twelve months from the appointed day, make such consequential, transitional or supplementary provisions as he may consider necessary consequent upon the repeal of the Petroleum Ordinance, or for the better bringing into effect of the objects and provisions of this Act.
