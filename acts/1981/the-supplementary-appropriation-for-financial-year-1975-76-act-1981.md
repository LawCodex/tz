# The Supplementary Appropriation (For Financial Year 1975-76) Act, 1981

NAKALA MTANDAO (ONLINE DOCUMENT)

TBE UNITED REPUBLIC OF TANZANIA

No. 4 OF 1981

President 22nd ....... MAY, 1981

An Act to apply a further sum of one hundred and eight million, six hundred twenty-seven thousand, and twenty-five shillings out of the Consolidated Fund to the Service of the year ending on the thirtieth day of June, one thousand nine hundred and seventy-six to appropriate the supply granted [ ENACTED by the Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania.

## Section 1

-(l) This Act may be cited as the Supplementary Appropriation (For Financial Year 1975 /76) (No. 2) Act, 198 1, and shall be read as one with the Appropriation Act, 1975.

(2) The expressions used m this Act shall each have the meaning ascribed to it in the Exchequer and Audit Ordinance.

## Section 2

The Treasury may issue out of the Consolidated Fund and apply towards the supply granted for the service of the year ending on the thirtieth day of June, 1976, the sum of one hundred and eight million, six hundred twenty-seven thousand, and twenty-five shillings.

## Section 3

The sum granted by section 2 shall be appropriated to the purposes and m the amounts specified in the second and fifth columns of the Schedule to this Act.

## Section 4

-(1) The Minister may at any time or times not later than the thirtieth day of June, 1976, borrow within or outside Tanzania, or Partly within and partly outside Tanzania, any sum or sums not exceeding in the whole one hundred and eight million, six hundred twenty-seven thousand, and twenty-five shillings by way of loan, advance, the issue of bills as bank overdraft and on such terms and conditions as he may deem expedient, and may charge any such loan or advance on any of the assets of the United Republic (including securities forming part of the Consolidated Fund). 1
