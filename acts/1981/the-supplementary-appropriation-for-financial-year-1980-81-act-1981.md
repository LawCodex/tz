An Act to apply a further sum of seventy-eight million, one hundred seventeen thousand, six hundred shillings out of the Consolidated Fund to the Service of the year ending on the thirtieth day of June, one thousand nine hundred and eighty-one and to appropriate the supply granted [ ENACTED by the Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania.

## Section 1

-(1) This Act may be cited as the Supplementary Appropriation (For Financial Year 1980/81) (No. 1) Act, 1981, and shall be read as one with the Appropriation Act, 1980.

(2) The expressions used in this Act shall each have the meaning ascribed to it in the Exchequer and Audit Ordinance.

## Section 2

The Treasury may issue out of the Consolidated Fund and apply towards the supply granted for the service of the year ending on the thirtieth day of June, 1981, the sum of Seventy-eight Million, one hundred seventeen thousand, six hundred shillings.

## Section 3

The sum granted by section 2 shall be appropriated to the purposes and in the amounts specified in the second and fifth column of the Schedule to this Act.
