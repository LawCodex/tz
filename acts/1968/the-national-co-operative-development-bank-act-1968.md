Section 35 of the National Co-operative and Development Bank Section 35 Act, 1964 is amended by deleting subsection (1) thereof and-substituting of Cap. 366 therefor the following subsection :—

“(1) Notwithstanding any provision to the.contrary in any written law no stamp duty or_registration or renewal fee or fee for filing a memorandum of satisfaction, shall-be chargeable in respect of any instrument or document.to which the Agency is a party in cases where, but for the exemption conferred by this subsection, the Agency or, where a co-operative society

, or an Agricultural Association is also a party to such instrument or document, the co-operative society or Agricultural Association, as the case may be, would be liable to pay the duty or fee chargeable in respect of such instrument or document.
