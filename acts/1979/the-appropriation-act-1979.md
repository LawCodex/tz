# The Appropriation Act, 1979

THE UNITED REPUBLIC OF TANZANIA

No. 11 OF 1979 I ASSENT,

9TH August, 1979

An Act to apply a sum of thirteen thousand nine hundred seventy. eight million, two hundred and eight thousand, six hundred shillings out of the Consolidated Fund to the Service of the year ending on the thirtieth day of June, 1980, to appropriate the supply granted for that year, to authorize the reallocation of certain appropriations and to provide for matters connected with those purposes. [

]

ENACTED by the Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania. I.-M This Act may be cited as the Appropriation Act, 1979.

(2) The expressions used in this Act shall have the meanings respectively ascribed to them in the Exchequer and Audit Ordinance.

Short title and interpretation Cap. 439

## Section 2

The Treasury may issue out of the Consolidated Fund and apply Issue of Shs. towards the supply granted for the service of the year ending on the 13,978,208, thirtieth day of June, 1980, the sum of thirteen thousand nine hundred 600/- out of seventy-eight million, two hundred and eight thousand, six hundred Consolidated Fund shillings.

## Section 3

The sum granted by section 2 shall be appropriated to the purposes and in the amounts specified in the second, third and fourth columns, respectively, of the First schedule to this Act.
