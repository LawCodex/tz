## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the Supplementary Appropriation Short title (For Financial Year 1986/87) (No.2) Act, 1994, and shall be read as and inter-pretation one with the Appropriation Act, 1986 Acts, 1986 No.9

## Section 2

The Treasury may issue out of the Consolidated Fund and Issue of apply towards the supply granted for the service of the year ending on the Shs. 382,863,809 thirtieth day of June, 1987, the sum of three hundred eighty-two milion, eight out of the hundred sixty-three thousand, eight hundred and nine shillings.
