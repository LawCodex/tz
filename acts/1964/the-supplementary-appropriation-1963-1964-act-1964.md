An Act to apply a further sum of seven hundred and eighty-six thousand, seven hundred and nineteen pounds out of the Consolidated Fund to the service of the year ending on the thirtieth day of June, one thousand nine hundred and sixty-four, and to appropriate the supply granted [29TH MAY, 1964] ENACTED by the Parliament of the United Republic of Tanganyika and Zanzibar. Short title

## Section 1

-(l) This Act may be cited as the Supplementary Appropriation and (1963-1964) (No. 2) Act, 1964. construction

(2) The expressions used in this Act shall have the meanings Ord. 1961 respectively ascribed thereto in the Exchequer and Audit Ordinance, No. 21 1961.

## Section 2

The Treasury may issue out of the Consolidated Fund and apply Issue of towards the supply granted for the service of the year ending on the £786,719 out of thirtieth day of June, 1964 the sum of seven hundred and eighty-six Consolidated Fund thousand, seven hundred and nineteen pounds.

## Section 3

The sum granted by section 2 shall be appropriated to the purposes Appropriation and in the amount specified in the second and third columns of the of money voted Schedule to this Act.

## Section 4

In addition to the sums granted out of the Consolidated Fund Appropriaunder section 2, there may be applied out of any moneys directed, tions-in-aid under section 9 of the Exchequer and Audit Ordinance, 1961, to be applied as appropriations-in-aid, the amounts respectively set forth in the fourth column of the Schedule to this Act.

## Section 5

-(1) The Minister may at any time or times not later than the Power of the thirtieth day of June, 1964, borrow within or outside Tanganyika, or Minister partly within and partly outside Tanganyika, any sum or sums not to borrow exceeding in the whole seven hundred and eighty-six thousand, seven hundred and nineteen pounds, by way of loan, advance, the issue of bills or bank overdraft and on such terms and conditions as the Minister may deem expedient, and may charge any such loan or advance on any of the assets of the United Republic (including securities forming part of the Consolidated Fund).
