# The Duration of Parliament (Extension) Act, 1964

NAKALA MTANDAO (ONLINE DOCUMENT)

THE UNITED REPUBLIC OF TANGANYIKA AND ZANZIBAR

No. 42 OF 1964

J

I ASSE

K. President 30 TH JULY, 1964

An Act to extend the life of the present Parliament [31ST JULY, 1964] ENACTED by the Parliament of the United Republic of Tanganyika and Zanzibar. 1 This Act may be cited as the Duration of Parliament (Extension) Short title Act, 1964, and shall be read as one with the Interim Constitution and and the Republic of Tanganyika (Consequential, Transitional and Temporary construction Provisions) Act, 1962.

## Section 2

-(1) Notwithstanding the provisions of subsection (3) of section Life of 44 of the Interim Constitution or of subsection (2) of section 19 of the present Republic of Tanganyika (Consequential, Transitional and Temporary Parliament by Provisions) Act, 1962, the present Parliament shall, unless sooner extended one year dissolved in accordance with any other provision of the Interim Con- C.A. Act 2 stitution, stand dissolved on the eleventh day of October, 1965: Provided that nothing in this subsection shall be constructed as precluding Parliament from extending the life of the present Parliament in accordance with the provisions of subsection (4) of section 44 of the Interim Constitution.

(2) Subsection (2) of section 19 of the said Act shall have effect in respect of any period commencing, or time, on or after the commencement of this Act as if for the year ''1964'' therein there were substituted the year ''1965''.

## Section 3

This Act shall extend to Zanzibar as well as Tanganyika. Zanzibar Passed in the National Assembly on the first day of July, 1964. Msekwa Clerk of the National Assembly
