## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Retirement (Special Provisions) and con(Amendment) Act, 1.964, and shall be read as one with the Retirement struction act 1962 (Special Provisions) Act, 1962 (hereinafter referred to as the principal no. 50 Act).

## Section 2

Subsection (1) of section 2 of the principal Act is hereby amended Section 2 of principal as follows: Act amended

(a) by deleting the definition ''African'';

(b) by inserting, m the appropriate alphabetical position, the following new definition: ''citizen' means a citizen of Tanganyika or of the United Republic;''-,

(c) by deleting item (ii) in the definition ''qualified officer'' and by substituting therefor the following new item: '' (ii) a citizen;''

## Section 3

Section 3 of the principal Act is hereby amended by deleting the Section 3 of principal substantive provisions (but not the proviso thereto) and by substituting Act amended therefor the following: ''If the President, or a Minister appointed in that behalf by the President, is satisfied that there are more candidates who are both citizens and qualified for appointment to,, or promotion in, any particular branch or category of officers in the service of the United Republic than there are vacancies in that category or branch that could be appropriately filled by such candidates, he may select qualified officers m that branch or category whose retirement would, in his opinion, cause vacancies that could appropriately be filled by such of those candidates as are available and fit for appointment, and, by notice m writing, --

Savings in respect of citizens who have been previously superseded

8,(I) Notwithstanding the amendment of the principal Act by this Act, any person who(a) was, before the commencement of this Act, entitled to retire from the public service under section 4 of the principal Act on the grounds that he had been superseded, before the fourteenth day of February, 1964, by an African; and

(b) gives notice of his intention to retire m accordance with the principal Act in sufficient time to enable him to retire or to proceed on leave pending retirement on or before the fourteenth day of February, 1965, shall not cease to be entitled to retire under that section solely on the ground that he is a citizen, but the principal Act (other than section 7 thereof) shall continue to apply to any such person as if this Act had not been passed: Provided that nothing in this subsection shall apply to any person who is registered or naturalized as a citizen in consequence of an application made after the commencement of this Act.

(2) The provisions of paragraph (b) of subsection (1) shall have effect m. relation to a person who is on overseas leave at any time between the fourteenth day of February, 1964, and the date of the commencement of this Act (both days inclusive), as if there were substituted for the fourteenth day of February, 1965,the day following the first anniversary of his return to duty from such leave.
