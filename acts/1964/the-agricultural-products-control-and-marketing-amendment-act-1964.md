# The Agricultural Products (Control and Marketing) (Amendment) Act, 1964

NAKALA MTANDAO (ONLINE DOCUMENT)

THE UNITED REPUBLIC OF TANZANIA

No. 66 OF 1964 I ASSENT,

K.

J

10 ..... TH DECEMBER, 1964

An Act to amend the Agricultural Products (Control and Marketing) Act, 1962 [11TH DECEMBER, 1964] ENACTED by the Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania.

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Agricultural Products (Control and Marketing) (Amendment) Act, 1964 and shall be read as one with the Agricultural Products (Control and Marketing) Act, 1962.

Short title and construction Act 1962 No. 56

## Section 2

The First Schedule to the Agricultural Products (Control and Marketing) Act, 1962, is hereby amended as follows: -

Amendment of first of First Schedule to Acts 1962 No. 56

(a) by adding, immediately below paragraph 7, the following new paragraph: ''7A. The Minister may direct that the, Chairman of a Board shall be a paid Chairman and, in any such case, he shall determine the salary, and the allowances and other benefits and conditions of service (if any), to be attached to the office of Chairman; and the Board shall pay and accord to the Chairman the salary, allowances and other benefits and conditions so determine" , and

(b) by adding, immediately below item (a) of sub-paragraph (1) of paragraph 22, the following new item: ''(aa) in any case in which the Minister has directed that the Chairman shall be a paid Chairman, payment of and for the Chairman to the extent determined by the Minister;"

NAKALA MTANDAO (ONLINE DOCUMENT)

No. 66

Confirmation of certain payments made to Chairman of National Agricultural Products Board

## Section 3

Payments made, before: the commencement of this Act and in accordance with the directions, of the Minister for commerce and Cooperatives, by the National Agricultural Products Board to and for the Chairman thereof are hereby authorized and confirmed.
