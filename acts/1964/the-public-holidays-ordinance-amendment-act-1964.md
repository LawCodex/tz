# The Public Holidays Ordinance (amendment) Act, 1964

NAKALA MTANDAO (ONLINE DOCUMENT) THE UNITED REPUBLIC OF TANZANIA No. 57 oF 1964 I ASSENT, (O.tH December, 1964 An Act to.amend the Public Holidays. Ordinance (11TH.DECEMBER, 1964]

ENACTED by the Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania.

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Public Holidays Ordinance short title (Amendment) Act, 1964, and shall be read as one with the. Public Cap. 35 Holidays Ordinance. ,
