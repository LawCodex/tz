ENACTED by the Parliament of the United’ Republic of Tanganyika and Zanzibar.

1, This Act may be:cited as the Export Tax Ordinance (Amendment) Short title Act, 1964, and shall.be read as one with the Export Tax Ordinance Or action (hereinafter referred:to as the Ordinance). Cap. 196
