# The National Agricultural Products Board Act, 1964

THE UNITED REPUBLIC OF TANGANYIKA AND ZANZIBAR

No. 39 OF 1964

J

I ASSE

K. President 30TH JULY, 1964

An Act to make further provision for, and to confer additional powers on, the National Agricultural Products Board, to modify the Agricultural Products (Control and Marketing) Act, 1962, in relation to the Board and the Products subject to its control and Powers of Regulation, to amend the said Act Generally, and for matters connected therewith and incidental thereto [31ST JULY, 1964]

WHEREAS. by an order made under section 3 of the Agricultural Products (Control and Marketing) Act, 1962, the Minister for Agriculture established a Board under the name of the National Agricultural Products Board for the control and regulation of maize throughout Tanganyika, and, by further orders under the said section, paddy. rice, maize flour, cashew nuts, simsim, castor, groundnuts and sunflower were added to the agricultural products subject to the Control and powers of regulation of the Board: AND WHEREAS it is considered appropriate and desirable that further provision should be made in relation to the Board and to agricultural products which are subject to its control and powers of regulation: Now THEREFORE BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the United Republic of Tanganyika and Zanzibar.

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the National Agricultural Products Board Act, 1964, and, subject to the provisions of section 2, shall be read as one with the Agricultural Products (Control and Marketing) Act, 1962 (hereinafter called ''the principal Act'').

## Section 2

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires' 'the Board'' means the National Agricultural Products Board; ''the Minister'' means the Minister for the time being responsible for commerce.

## Section 3

-(1) Notwithstanding any of the orders made under section 3 of the principal Act in relation to the Board or to any of the agricultural products set out in the Schedule to this Act, the whole of Tanganyika shall be deemed to have been declared an area in which the marketing of the said agricultural products shall be controlled and regulated and the Board shall be deemed to have been established for the control and regulation of the marketing of the said agricultural products within the whole of Tanganyika; and notwithstanding that the Board is deemed to have been established for the control and regulation of the marketing only of the said or any other agricultural products, it shall have all the powers of a Board under the principal Act: Provided that the Board may, with the prior approval of the Minister and by order published in the Gazette, exclude any area or areas from the operation of its control and powers of regulation, and with the like approval and in the like manner revoke or amend any such order.

(2) Where any specified agricultural product which is subject to the control and Powers of regulation of the Board is also subject to any control or powers of regulation of any other Board established under the principal Act, and there is any conflict between the orders, directions or other instructions of the Board and those of such other Board, the orders, directions and instructions of the Board shall prevail, and where both the Board and any such other Board impose a levy on the same specified agricultural product, the imposition of such levy by such other Board shall, to that extent, be void.

## Section 4

The Minister(a) shall not have power to dissolve the Board;

(b) shall not, without the prior approval of the National Assembly signified by resolution, declare the Board to be deemed to have been established for the control and regulation of any additional specified agricultural product or remove any specified agricultural product from the control or powers of regulation of the Board: Provided that, if the Minister is of the opinion that circumstances urgently require that the marketing of any agricultural product used for human consumption should be controlled and regulated, he may, by order under section 3 of the principal Act, declare the Board to be deemed to have been established for the control and regulation thereof, but any such order shall be subject to the approval of the National Assembly at its next meeting and, if not then approved, shall expire. The expire of such an order shall be without prejudice to the validity of anything done or suffered thereunder.

## Section 6

-(1) In addition to the powers conferred on the Board under the principal Act as modified by this Act, the Board shall have power(a) with the prior approval of the Minister, to make orders prohibiting any dealing in, barter, offer for sale, sale, purchase or hire of the specified agricultural product or its derivatives except by the Board, its agents. persons registered or licensed by the Board, or, in the case of an offer for sale or sale, the producers thereof, and restricting or controlling such activities by any person not subject to any such prohibition;

(b) with the prior approval of the Minister, to make orders prohibiting the transport. storage or processing of the specified agricultural product or its derivatives except by the Board, its agents, persons registered or licensed by the Board or, in the case of storage, the producers thereof, and restricting or controlling such activities by any person not subject to any such prohibition:

(c) with the prior approval of the Minister, to make orders prescribing the specifications to which any factory, building or plant for the processing or storage of the specified agricultural product or its derivatives may be erected and of any machinery installed therein, the conditions upon which the same may be erected, installed. maintained and used, and the buildings in which such machinery may be housed;

(d) where the Board has made a compulsory marketing order. to make orders prohibiting the barter or offering for sale of the specified product in the area to which such compulsory marketing order relates except in conformity with the compulsory marketing order;

(e) to make orders calling for the submission of returns or information relating to production, stocks, processing, transport, purchase or sale of the specified agricultural product or its derivatives,

(f) to make orders that the specified agricultural product or its derivatives shall be stored in such conditions or manner as the Board or any person authorized in that behalf by the Board may direct: Provided that nothing in the foregoing provisions of this subsection shall apply to the retail sale or offer for sale of the processed derivatives of the specified agricultural product in quantities not greater than those that may be prescribed or for the consumption or use of the purchaser and his household.

## Section 7

-(1) The Board shall establish and maintain, in accordance with Export the provisions of this section, an export trading equalization fund for trading each specified agricultural product which is subject to its control or equalization fund powers of regulation and which is exported or sold for export by the Board outside the area comprising the United Republic of Tanganyika and Zanzibar.

(2) There shall be paid into each such fund maintained by the Board(a) such sums as may be provided by Parliament;

(b) the sum (if any) by which the proceeds from such export or sale for export of the specified agricultural product outside the said area exceeds the aggregate of the sums expended by the Board by way of purchase, expenses, costs, interest and other charges on the amount of the product so sold and exported.

(3) In addition to the sums provided for m subsection (2), the Board may, and shall if so directed by the Minister, pay into any such fund maintained for a specified agricultural product, any profit of the Board from the sale of that specified agricultural product within the said area.

(4) If in any financial year the proceeds from the export and sale for export by the Board of any specified agricultural product outside the said area are less than the aggregate of the sums expended by the Board as aforesaid on the amounts of the product so exported or sold for export, the Board shall, to the extent that there are monies available in the export trading equalization fund maintained for that product, transfer from such fund to its general funds the amount by which the aggregate of such sums exceeds the proceeds from such export and sale for export.

## Section 8

Subject to any orders made or directions given under the principal Cleaning Act or this Act, any person authorized in writing in that behalf may, upon of storage production of such authorization, require the occupier of any land or premises used for the storage, processing or packing of the specified agricultural product or its derivatives, or the person in charge of any vehicle used for its or their transport, to clean and disinfest the same, and any person so required who fails, without reasonable cause, to clean or disinfest such land, premises or vehicle shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable upon conviction to a fine not exceeding five hundred shillings or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding three months or to both such fine and imprisonment, or, in the case of a second or subsequent offence, to a fine not exceeding two thousand shillings or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months or to both such fine and imprisonment.

## Section 9

-(1) The Price Controller may, with the prior approval of the Minister and by order published in the Gazette, fix the minimum price at which any specified agricultural product which is subject to the control or powers of regulation of the Board or any derivative thereof may be purchased at any stage of the marketing thereof.

(2) Any person who purchases any of the specified agricultural product or any derivative thereof in circumstances in which such order applies at a lesser price than, that prescribed by such order shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding two thousand shillings or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months or to both such fine and imprisonment or, in the case of a second or subsequent offence, to a fine not exceeding four thousand shillings or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding one year or to both such fine and imprisonment.

(3) In this section, ''the Price Controller'' means the Price Controller appointed under the Price Control Ordinance.
