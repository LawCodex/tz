THE WRITTEN LAWS (MISCELLANEOUS AMENDMENTS) ACT, 2018

I ASSENT DR. JOHN POMBE JOSEPH MAGUFULI President [8th February, 2018] An Act to amend certain written laws. ENACTED by Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania.

## PART I — PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS

Short title

### Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Written Laws (Miscellaneous Amendments) Act, 2018.

Amendment of certain written laws

### Section 2

The Written Laws specified in various Parts of this Act are amended in the manner specified in their respective Parts.
