## PART I — PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS

Short title Cap.103

### Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Public Private Partnership (Amendment) Act, 2018 and shall be read as one with the Public Private Partnership Act, hereinafter referred to as the “principal Act”.
