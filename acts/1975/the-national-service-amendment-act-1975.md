This Act may be cited as the National Service (Amendment) Act. 1975, shall be read as one with the National Service Act, 1964 (hereinafter referred to as ''the principal Act'') and shall be deemed to have come into operation on the first day of July, 1975.

## Section 22A

(b) wears a distinctive mark relating to wounds received or service performed in any National Service operation, or a medal, ribbon, badge, chevron or any decoration or order that is awarded in relation to any service in the National Service, or any imitation thereof, or any mark or device or thing that is likely to be mistaken for any such mark, medal, ribbon, badge, chevron, decoration or order;

## Section 22B

Nothing in section 22 or 22A shall prohibit(a) the use or wearing of National Service badges for purposes of ornament, or the wearing of brooches or other ornaments representing such military badges; or

(b) the wearing or use of such uniforms or things as are referred to in section 22 and 22A for purposes of any theatrical performance.''.

## Section 22

Consequent upon the abolition of the permanent staff of the National Service by amendments to the principal Act introduced by this Act, every member of the permanent staff shall, with effect from the date on which this Act is deemed to have come into operation, be appointed to such rank in the Tanzania Peoples Defence Forces as the appropriate authority having the power to make such appointment may determine.
