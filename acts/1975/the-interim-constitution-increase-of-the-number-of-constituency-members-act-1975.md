# The Interim Constitution (Increase of the Number of Constituency Members) Act, 1975

NAKALA MTANDAO (ONLINE DOCUMENT)

THE UNITED REPUBLIC OF TANZANIA

No. 10 OF 1975

I ASSENT,

.

10TH

JULY, 1975

An Act to increase the number of Constituency Members [

JULY, 1975]

ENACTED by the Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania.

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Interim Constitution (Increase of the Number of Constituency Members) Act, 1975 and shall be read as one with the Interim Constitution of Tanzania, 1965

Short title

## Section 2

The number of constituency members provided for m subsection

(1) of section 24 of the Interim Constitution of Tanzania, 1965 is hereby increased from eighty-eight to ninety-six.

Increase of the number of constituency members Consequential provision

## Section 3

This Act being enacted pursuant to the provisions of subsection

(3) of section 24 of the Interim Constitution of Tanzania, 1965, it shall have the effect of altering the relevant provisions of the said section 24 and the Interim Constitution of Tanzania, 1965 shall take effect as if the number of constituency members provided for therein were, ninety-six. Passed in the National Assembly on the fourth day of July, 1975. maina Clerk of the National Assembly
