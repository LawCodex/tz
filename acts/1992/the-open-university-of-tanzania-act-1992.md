(5) i The Inquiry Officer shall notify the accused student of the day, date, time and place at which the inquiry shall be held. The inquiry shall not be open to the public.

(7) The accused student shall have the right(a) to cross-examine any witness examined by the Inquiry Officer or by the disciplinary authority;

(b) to examine and make copies of any document produced as evidence against him;
