THE WRITTEN LAWS (MISCELLANEOUS AMENDMENTS) ACT, 2017 I ASSENT JOHN POMBE JOSEPH MAGUFULI, President [21st February, 2017]

An Act to amend certain written laws. ENACTED by Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania.

## PART I — PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS

Short title

### Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Written Laws (Miscellaneous Amendments) Act, 2017.

Amendment of certain written laws

### Section 2

The Written laws specified in various Parts of this Act are amended in the manner specified in their respective Parts.
