Establishment of Commission

Functions of Commission

21.-(l) There is hereby established a Commission to be known as the Presidential Parastatal Sector Reform Commission which shall be an autonomous organ of the Government.

(2) The Commission shall be a body corporate with perpetual succession and common seal and shall be capable in its corporate name of

(a) suing and being sued;
