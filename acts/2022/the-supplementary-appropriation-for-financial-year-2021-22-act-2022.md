# The Supplementary Appropriation (For Financial Year 2021/22) Act, 2022

ISSN 0856 - 034X THE UNITED REPUBLIC OF TANZANIA 8th March, 2022

Supplement No. 3

ACT SUPPLEMENT to the Gazette of the United Republic of Tanzania No. 3 Vol.103 dated 8th March, 2022 Printed by the Government Printer, Dodoma by Order of Government

THE SUPPLEMENTARY APPROPRIATION (FOR FINANCIAL YEAR 2021/22) ACT, 2022 I ASSENT SAMIA SULUHU HASSAN President [23rd February, 2022] An Act to apply a sum of One Trillion, Three Hundred Ten Billion, Six Hundred Fifty Million, Two Hundred and Nine Thousand Shillings out of the Consolidated Fund to the Service of the year ending the thirtieth day of June, 2022, to appropriate the supply granted. ENACTED by Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania. Short title Act No. 2 of 2021

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Supplementary Appropriation (for Financial Year 2021/22) Act, 2022 and shall be read together with Appropriation Act, 2021.

Interpretation Cap. 348 Cap. 439

## Section 2

The expressions used in this Act shall each have the meaning ascribed to them in the Public Finance Act and the Budget Act.

Issuance of Shs. 1,310,650,209,000 out of Consolidated Fund

## Section 3

The Treasury may issue out of the Consolidated Fund and apply towards the supply granted for the service of the year ending on the thirtieth day of June, 2022, the sum of One Trillion, Three Hundred Ten

Billion, Six Hundred Fifty Million, Two Hundred and Nine Thousand Shillings. Appropriation of moneys voted

## Section 4

The sum granted under section 3 shall be appropriated for the purposes and in the amounts specified in the fourth and fifth columns of the Schedule to this Act.
