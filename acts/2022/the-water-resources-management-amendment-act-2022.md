THE WATER RESOURCES MANAGEMENT (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2022 I ASSENT SAMIA SULUHU HASSAN,

President [30th September, 2022]

An Act to amend the Water Resources Management Act. ENACTED by the Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania.

## PART I — PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS

Short title Cap. 331

### Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Water Resources Management (Amendment) Act, 2022, and shall be read as one with the Water Resources Management Act, 2009 hereinafter referred to as the “principal Act”.
