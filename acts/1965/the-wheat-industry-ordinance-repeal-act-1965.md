# The Wheat Industry Ordinance (Repeal) Act, 1965

NAKALA MTANDAO (ONLINE DOCUMENT) THE UNITED REPUBLIC OF TANZANIA No. 34 oF 1965 I ASSENT, corn a ee Prresident 8tH Jury, 1965 An Act to repeal the Wheat Industry Ordinance

ENACTED by the Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania.

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Wheat Industry Ordinance (Repeal) Short title Act, 1965 and shall come into operation on such date.as the Minister tencement responsible for commerce shall, by notice published in the Gazette, appoint.

## Section 2

The Wheat Industry, Ordinance is hereby repealed. Repeal of

‘ap.

Passed in the National Assembly.on the thirtieth day of June, 1965.
