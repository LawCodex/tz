## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Land Acquisition Ordinance (Amend- Short title ment) Act, 1965 and shall be read as one with the Land Acquisition and construction Ordinance. Cap. 118

## Section 2

The Land Acquisition Ordinance is hereby amended as follows:- Extension

(a) in the long tide thereof, by adding immediately after the word of ambit of ''Purposes'', the words ''and in connection with rehousing Cap. 118 schemes'';

(b) by inserting, immediately above section 2, the following new heading:''PART I ACQUISITION FOR PUBLIC PURPOSES''

## Section 3

Section 2 of the Land Acquisition Ordinance is hereby amended by deleting paragraph (a) of the definition ''public purpose'' and by substituting therefor the following new paragraph: ''(a) for exclusive Government use, for general public use, for any Government scheme for the development of agricultural land, or for the provision of sites for industrial or commercial development, social services or housing;''
