# The Aerodromes Control Act,1965

NAKALA MTANDAO (ONLINE DOCUMENT) THE UNITED REPUBLIC OF TANZANIA 7 UNGN AN No. 8 oF 1965 Fibs K Qo Marcu, 1965 An Act to control the entry of persons, animals and vehicles upon licensed acrodromes [26TH Marcu, 1965] ENACTED by the Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania. 1, This Act may be cited as the Aerodromes Control Act, 1965. Short title

## Section 2

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires— Interpretation “licensed aerodrome” means a Government aerodrome or an aerodrome licensed, under any law in force in Tanzania; “Minister” means the Minister for the time being responsible for civil aviation.

## Section 3

—(1) The Ministersmay make regulations, “not inconsistent with Regulations any order or regulation made under any other law in force in Tanzania regulating licensed aerodromes, for prohibiting, restricting or controlling the entry of any person, animal_or vehicle»to a licensed aerodrome or ; any part thereof, and for controlling the movement of any person, animal or vehicle within such licensed aerodrome.

(2) Regulations made under this section may be applied generally or to any specified licensed aerodrome or aerodromes.

(3) Without prejudice to the generality of the foregoing, regulations made under this section may—

(a) prescribe fees to be paid by any person or in respect of any animal or vehicle entering or remaining on a licensed aerodrome, or any part thereof;

(b) provide in respect Of a breach of the provisions thereof penalties not exceeding a fine of four thousand shillings or imprisonment for a term of six months or both such fine and imprisonment;
