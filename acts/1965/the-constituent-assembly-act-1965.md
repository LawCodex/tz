An Act to extend the time for the summoning of a Constituent Assembly [26TH MARCH, 1965] ENACTED by the Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania.

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Constituent Assembly Act, 1965 and shall be read as one with the Acts of Union of Tanganyika and Zanzibar.

Short title and construction Acts 1964 No. 22 G.N 1964 No. 243
