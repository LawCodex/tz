## Section 1

-(1) This Act may be cited as the Supplementary Appropriation (No. 3 for the Financial Year 1972/73) (No. 3) Act, 1973 and shall be read as one with the Appropriation Act, 1972.

(2) The expressions used m this Act shall have the meanings respectively ascribed thereto in the Exchequer and Audit Ordinance.
