# The Industrial Shares (Acquisition) Act, 1973

NAKALA MTANDAO (ONLINE DOCUMENT) THE UNITED REPUBLIC OF TANZANIA nN : be No. 13 oF 1973 IT ASSENT, Se den 23RD JULY, 1973 An Act to amend the Industrial, Shares (Acquisition) Act, 1967 [19TH JUNE, 1973]

ENACTED by the Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania.

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Industrial Shares (Acquisition) Short title (Amendment) Act, 1973, shall be read as one with the Industrial 44. 1967 Shares (Acquisition) Act, 1967, and shall be deemed to have come into No. 5 operation on the nineteenth day of June, 1973.

## Section 2

Section 3 ofthe Industrial Shares (Acquisition) Act, 1967 is Section 3 amended by deleting subsection (1A) and substituting. therefor the amended following subsection :——

“(1a) Notwithstanding the provisions.of subsection (1) an order under that subsection may be made in respect of the company. specified in the Schedulesto this Act as Tanzania Bata Shoe Company Limited, which company is now called Tanzania Shoe Company.Limited, and also in respect of Tanganyika Portland Cement Company Limited, vesting in the Treasury Registrar all the shares in those companies either by the same order or by any subsequent order.”’.

Passed in the National Assembly on the eighteenth fe ly, 1973.
