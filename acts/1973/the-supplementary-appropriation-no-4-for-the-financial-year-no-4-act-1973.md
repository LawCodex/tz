# The Supplementary Appropriation (No.4) for the Financial Year (No.4) Act, 1973

NAKALA MTANDAO (ONLINE DOCUMENT)

THE UNITED REPUBLIC OF TANZANIA

No. 8 OF 1973

1, RASHIDI M. KAWAWA, having been appointed by the President to discharge the Functions of the Office of the President during his absence, assent.

Prime Minister and Second Vice-President 30TH JUNE, 1973

An Act to apply a further sum of twenty-seven million, eight hundred fifty eight thousand, two hundred and seven shillings out of the Consolidated Fund to the service of the year which ended on the thirtieth day of June, one thousand nine hundred and seventy two and to appropriate the supply granted [30TH JUNE, 1973] ENACTED by the Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania.

## Section 1

-(1) This Act may be cited as the Supplementary Appropriation (No. 4 for the Financial Year 1971/72 (No. 4) Act, 1973, and shall be read as one with the Appropriation Act, 1971.

(2) The expressions used in this Act shall have the meanings respectively ascribed thereto in the Exchequer and Audit Ordinance.

Short title and interpretation Acts 1972 No. 19 Cap. 439

## Section 2

The Treasury may issue out of the Consolidated Fund and apply towards the supply granted for the service of the year which ended on the thirtieth day of June, 1972 a sum of twenty seven million eight hundred fifty eight thousand two hundred and seven shillings.

Issue of Shs. 27,858,207 out of Consolidated Fund

## Section 3

The sum granted by section 2 shall be appropriated to the purposes and in the amounts specified m the second and fifth columns of the Schedule to this Act.
