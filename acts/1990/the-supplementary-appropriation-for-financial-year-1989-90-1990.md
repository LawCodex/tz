-(1) The Minister may at any time or times not later than the thirtieth day of June, 1990, borrow within or outside Tanzania or partly within and partly outside Tanzania, any sum or sums not exceeding in the whole eight thousand two hundred fourteen million, one hundred eighty-one thousand nine hundred shillings by way of loan, advance, the issue of bill as bank overdraft and on such terms and conditions as he may deem expedient, and may charge any such loan or advance on any of the assets of the United Republic (including securities forming part of the Consolidated Fund).

(2) Any money borrowed under this section shall be placed to the credit of . he exchequer account and shall form part of the Consolidated Fund, and be available in any manner in which that Fund is available.

(3) Any money borrowed under this section shall be subject to repayment not later than the thirty-first day of October, 1990, and any sum required for the payment of any money so borrowed, and of any interest payable thereon shall be charged on and paid out of the Consolidated Fund.
