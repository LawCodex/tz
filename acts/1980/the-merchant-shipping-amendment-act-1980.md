# The Merchant Shipping (Amendment) Act, 1980

NAKALA MTANDAO (ONLINE DOCUMENT)

THE UNITED REPUBLIC OF TANZANIA

No. 21 OF 1980

President

8TH September, 1980 ................................ An Act to amend the Merchant Shipping Act, 1967, to make provision for the training and certification of seamen and the manning of ships. [

]

ENACTED by the Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania.

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Merchant Shipping (Amendment) Act, 1980, shall be read as one with the Merchant Shipping Act, 1967, and shall come into operation on such date as the Minister may, by notice in the Gazette, appoint.

## Section 2

Section 2 of the Merchant Shipping Act, 1967 is amended by deleting subsection (2) and substituting for it the following: ''(2) Subject to Section 81B Of this Act, this Act Shall not apply to the regulation of inland water transport.''

## Section 3
