# The Supplementary Appropriation Act, 1979 No. 11 (For Financial Year 1979-80) Act, 1980

NAKALA MTANDAO (ONLINE DOCUMENT)

THE UNITED REPUBLIC OF TANZANIA

No. 16 OF 1980

President 21ST MAY, 1980 .........

An Act to apply a further sum of four hundred sixty-two million, five hundred fifty-five thousand, two hundred shillings out of the Consolidated Fund to the Service of the year ending on the thirtieth day of June, one thousand nine hundred and eighty and to appropriate the supply granted [

]

ENACTED by the Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania.

## Section 1

-(1) This Act may be cited as the Supplementary Appropriation Acts, 1979 No. 11 (For Financial Year 1979/80) Act, 1980, and shall read as one with the Appropriation Act, 1979.

Short title and interpretation

(2) The expressions used in this Act shall each have the Meaning ascribed to it in the Exchequer and Audit Ordinance.

Cap. 439

## Section 2

The Treasury may issue out of the Consolidated Fund and apply towards the supply granted for the service of the year ending on the thirtieth day of June, 1980, the sum of four hundred sixty-two million, five hundred fifty-five thousand, two hundred shillings.

Issue of Shs. 465,555,200 out of the Consolidated

## Section 3

The sum granted by section 2 shall be appropriated to the purposes, and in the amounts specified m the second and fifth columns of the Schedule to this Act.
