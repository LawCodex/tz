# The Tanzania Livestock Research Organization Act, 1980

THE TANZANIA LIVESTOCK RESEARCH ORGANIZATION ACT, 1980 An Act to establish the Tanzania Livestock Research Organization, to provide for its functions and for matters relating to the promotion of research on livestock [ ENACTED by the Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania,

]

## PART I — PRELIMINARY

### Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Tanzania Livestock Research Organiza- Short title tion Act, 1980, and shall come into operation on such date as the Minister and commencement may by notice in the Gazette, appoint.

### Section 2

In this Act unless the context requires otherwise"centre'' means a centre of an institute established under section 5; ''The Council'' means the Council of the Organization established under section 3; 'Director'' means a Director of an institute appointed under section 9; ''Director-General'' means the Director-General of the Organization appointed under section 8; ''Director of Research'' means the Director of Research of the OrganizaCap. 156 tion appointed under section 9; ''disease'' means any disease specified under section 2 of the Animal Diseases Ordinance. ''Institute'' means an institute of the Organization established by or under section 5;

• 'Livestock'' means cattle, sheep, goats, pigs, and includes poultry; ''Minister'' means the Minister for the time being responsible for Agriculture; ''the Organization'' means the Tanzania Livestock Organization established by section 3.

### Section 4

-(1) The functions of the Organization shall be(a) to promote the development improvement and protection of livestock in the United Republic;

(b) to carry out, and promote the carrying out of investigations, experiments and research for the purposes of promoting the development of the livestock industry;

(c) to continue, develop and finalize all on-going research on livestock in the United Republic:

(d) to carry out research and investigation into diseases affecting livestock including their causes, methods of treating, preventing or controlling them;

(e) to carry out research in and to undertake the preparation of vaccines for livestock, and to recommend and certify vaccines suitable for use in the livestock industry in the United Republic;

(f) to carry out research in livestock nutrition including research in pastures and animal feed stuffs;

(g) to carry out research, experiments and investigation s in livestock for the production of animal feed stuffs or for any other useful purpose.

(h) to carry out research, experiments and investigations in livestock breeding including the production of improved and high Yield breeds;

(i) to co-ordinate all research relating to the livestock industry carried on in the United Republic;

(j) to establish and operate a system of documentation and dissemination of the findings of investigations, experiments and research carried out by or on behalf of the Organization;

(k) to collect, publish and distribute statistics and other information relating to the livestock industry and to establish or operate a library for that purpose;

(1) in co-operation with the Government or any person within or outside the United Republic, to promote and provide facilities for the instruction and training of local personnel for carrying out research on livestock;

(m) to advise the Government, public institutions, villages or persons engaged in livestock breeding in the United Republic, on the practical application of findings of investigations, experiments and research carried out by of on behalf of the Organization;

(n) to establish demonstration centres for the purposes of educating individuals and institutions in animal husbandry and modern methods of livestock breeding;

## PART IV — OPERATION AND CONTROL OF RESEARCH

### Section 20

-(1) The Council shall appoint a research programmes committee which shall, subject to any general or specific directions which the Council may give in that behalf, be responsible, for the preparation of research programmes of the Organization and for the scrutiny of proposals made to or by the Organization.

(2) The Council may appoint such number of other committees or sub-committees as it may deem necessary for the formulation, control and co-ordination of research programmes proposed to be carried out under this Act.

(3) Subject to the provisions of Section 27 the Council may upon such conditions as it may specify, delegate to any committee or sub-committee appointed under this section all or any of its functions under this Act.

Research Programmes Committee, etc

### Section 21

-(1) In recommending or determining whether or not any research programme be carried out by the Organization the Council, shall take into account-

Evaluation of research programmes

(a) the necessity or desirability and propriety of the proposed research programme;

(b) the anticipated cost of the proposed research programme;

(c) the ability of the Organization to carry out the proposed research having regard to any other research programmes being carried out by the Organization.

(2) The Council may, after considering the factors specified in subsection (3) in respect of any research programme referred to it, approve or disapprove the research programme as presented or with its approval and require further information or particulars in connection with that programme from the Director-General or Committee.

### Section 23

### Section 24

-(1) All information furnished to the Organization pursuant to section 22 or 23 shall be kept by the Organization for the purposes of record, and may be deal with or disposed of in such manner or for such purposes as the Minister may approve or direct.

(2) Save where the Minister directs otherwise, all information furni shed to the Organization pursuant to section 22 or 23 shall be treated as confidential.

(3) Subject to subsection (4), any member of the Council or employee of the Organization who discloses to any person, except for the purposes of the performance of his duties or exercise of his powers under this Act, any information furnished to the Organization pursuant to section 22 or 23 or any confidential information which was in any other way acquired by the member of the Council or employee of the Organization in the performance of his duties under this Act, shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding three thousand shillings to imprisonment for a term not exceeding twelve months or to both that fine and imprisonment.

(4) Subsection (3) shall not apply to the disclosure of information made for the purposes of legal proceedings instituted under this Act or under any written law or for the purposes of a report relating to those proceedings.

(5) Where the Organization furnishes to any person or institution any information of a confidential nature, subsection (4) of this section shall apply to that person or institution as if references in that section to a member of the Council and employee of the Organization, were references to that person or institution.
