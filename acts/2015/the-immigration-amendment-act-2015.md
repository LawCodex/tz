## PART III — POWERS, RIGHTS AND DUTIES OF IMMIGRATION OFFICERS

General duties of the Service

### Section 12

-(1) The general duties and responsibilities of immigration officers shall be to(a) manage and monitor entry and exit of migrants;

(b) safeguard national borders;

(c) facilitate and manage movement of foreigners wishing to enter, stay and reside in the United Republic of Tanzania.

### Section 16

An immigration officer shall have powers to investigate and make inquiry on any person who has contravened or he has reasonable cause to believe that the person is or about to contravene any provisions of this Act or any other written law.

### Section 17

-(1) An immigration officer may, without warrant, arrest any person who has committed or who has reasonable cause to believe is about to commit an offence under this Act or any other written laws, provided that the person arrested under this section shall be brought before a magistrate as soon as is practicable.

(2) An immigration officer shall not use excessive force in exercising the powers conferred by subsection (1).
