THE UNITED REPUBLIC OF TANZANIA

No. 18 OF 1972 I ASSENT,

17TH AUGUST, 1972

An Act to impose and alter certain Taxes and Duties and to amend certain Enactments relating to Taxes and Duties and for purposes connected therewith [16TH JUNE, 1972] ENACTED by the Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania. 1. This Act may be cited as the Finance Act, 1972.
