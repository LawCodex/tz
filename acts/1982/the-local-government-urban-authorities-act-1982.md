### Section 19

-(1) Every town council shall consist of(a) members elected one each from the wards within the town;

(b) the Member of Parliament representing the constituency within which the town is situated;

(c) if he is resident in the area of the council, the national Member of Parliament elected from the region in which the council is situated;

(d) not more than five other members appointed by the Minister from among the residents of the town.

(2) Every municipal council shall consist of(a) members elected one each from the wards within the municipality;

(d) not more than five other members appointed by the Minister from among the residents the municipality.
