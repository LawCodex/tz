## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the National Industries (Licensing and Registration) (Amendment) Act, 1982, shall be read as one with the National Industries (Licensing and Registration) Act, 1967 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act) and shall come into operation on such date as the Minister may, by Order in the Gazette, appoint.
