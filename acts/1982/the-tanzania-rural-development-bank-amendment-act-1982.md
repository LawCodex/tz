Act to amend the Tanzania Rural Development Bank Act, 1971 in order to allow the apex organisation to subscribe for shares in the Bank [

]

ENACTED by the Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania.

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Tanzania Rural Development Bank (Amendment) Act, 1982, and shall be read as one with the Tanzania Rural Development Bank Act, 1971 (in this Act referred to as the principal Act) and shall come into operation on such date as the Minister may, by order published in the Gazette, appoint.

## Section 2

Section 2 of the principal Act is hereby amended by- ,

(a) deleting the expression ''parastatal Organisation'' and its definition;

Short title construction and commerncement Acts, 1971 No. 7 Section 2 amended

(b) inserting the following definitions in their respective appropriate alphabetical order; that is to say" apex organisation'' means a co-operative society the membership of which is restricted to secondary societies and which is established to facilitate the operations of all primary and secondary societies; "co-operative society'' has the same meaning ascribed to it under the Co-operative' Societies Act, 1982.1

## Section 3

Section 6 of the principal Act is amended(a) in subsection (4) by deleting the phrase '' a parastatal organisation'', and substituting for it the phrase ''the apex organisation registered under the Co-operative Societies Act, 1982'';(b) in subsection (7), by deleting the phrase ''any parastatal organisation named by the President in such direction'', and substituting for it the phrase ''the apex organisation''
