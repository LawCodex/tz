# The Kagera Transport Assets, (Acquisition and Regrant) Act, 1984

THE KAGERA TRANSPORT ASSETS (ACQUISITION AND REGRANT) ACT, 1983 An Act to give statutory effect to the acquisition, and provide for the regrant and transfer of management, of certain transport assets in Kagera Region and for other incidental or related matters WuereaS in consequence of the implementation of the provisions of the Economic Sabotage (Special Provisions) Act, 1983, many vehicles and other articles related to transport were brought into the custody of the police: AND WHEREAS such action resulted in the virtual stoppage of transport activities within the Kagera Region: ; AND WHEREAS, subsequently, the Government of the United Republic directed that certain of the transport assets be acquired and vested in the Treasury Registrar as from the twenty-second day of August, 1983: AND WHEREAS, consequent upon the acquisition of the transport assets it is meet to give statutory effect to that acquisition and to make other provisions to facilitate the more effectual take over and management of the said transport assets: Now, THEREFORE, BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania as follows:
