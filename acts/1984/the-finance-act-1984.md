# The Finance Act, 1984

THE FINANCE BILL, 1984 An Act to impose and to alter certain taxes, to amend certain written financial laws and to provide for other. connected matters ENACTED by the Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania.

1\. This Act may be cited as the Finance Act, 1984, 2. Save where it is expressly provided to the contrary in respect of the commencement of any Part of this Act, this Act shall be deemed to have come into operation on the fifteenth day of June, 1984.
