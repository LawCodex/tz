# The Supplementary Appropriation (1963 - 1964) (No. 1) Act, 1963

NAKALA MTANDAO (ONLINE DOCUMENT)

TANGANYIKA

No. 52 OF 1963 I ASSENT, Julius K. Nyerere President 19 TH SEPTEMBER, 1963

An Act to apply a further sum of two hundred and fourteen thousand, three hundred and seventy-five pounds out of the Consolidated Fund to the service of the year ending on the thirtieth day of June, one thousand nine hundred and sixty-four, and to appropriate the supply granted [20TH SEPTEMBER, 1963]

ENACTED by the Parliament of Tanganyika. title

## Section 1

-(1) This Act may be cited as the Supplementary Appropriation Short and (1963-1964) (No. 1) Act, 1963. construction

(2) The expressions used in. this Act shall have the meanings Ord. 1961 respectively ascribed thereto in the Exchequer and Audit Ordinance. No. 21 1961. I

## Section 2

The Treasury may issue out of the Consolidated Fund and apply towards the supply granted for the service of the year ending on the thirtieth day of June, 1964 the sum of two hundred and fourteen thousand, three hundred and seventy-five pounds.

Issue of pound 214,375 out of Consolidated Fund

## Section 3

The sum granted by section, 2 shall be appropriated to the purposes Appropriation of and in the amounts specified in the second and third columns Of the money voted Schedule to this Act. Appropriations

## Section 4

In addition to the sums granted out of the Consolidated Fund under inaid section 2, there may be applied out of any moneys directed, under section 9 of the Exchequer and Audit Ordinance, 1961, to be applied as appropriations in aid, the amounts respectively set forth in the fourth column of the Schedule to this Act.
