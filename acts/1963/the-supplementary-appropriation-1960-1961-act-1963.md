# The Supplementary Appropriation (1960 - 1961) Act, 1963

NAKALA MTANDAO (ONLINE DOCUMENT) TANGANYIKA © KO & S47, No. 8 oF 1963 I ASSENT, K[lywe pT Marcu, 1963 An Act to make further provision for the Public Service for the period of twelve months) ended on the thirtieth day of June, one thousand nine hundred and sixty-one [8ta Marcu, 1963]

Whereas by the Appropriation Ordinance, 1960 a sum not exceeding ord. 1960 twenty-six million; nine hundred and ‘eighty-three thousand, three No. 14 hundred and seventy-nine pounds was authorized to be paid out of the revenue and other funds of Tanganyika for the service of Tanganyika for the period of twelve months ended on the thirtieth day of June, one thousand nine hundred and sixty-one to be appropriated in conformity with the Schedule to that Ordinance.

And whereas the expenditure in respect of certain of the.services set forth in the said Schedule during the said period exceeded the sum so authorized as aforesaid by one. million, one-hundred and ninety-nine thousand, seven hundred) and-seventy-five pounds under the heads of expenditure detailed in the Schedule to-this Act. '

BE IT THEREFORE ENACTED by the Parliament of Tanganyika.

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Supplementary Appropriation (1960- Short title 61) Act, 1963.

## Section 2

The public revenue for the period of twelve months ended on the Public thirtieth day of June, one thousand nine hundred and sixty-one and the {itses other funds of Tanganyika are hereby charged towards the services for the said period as specified in the Schedule hereto with a further sum of one million, one hundred and ninety-nine thousand, seven hundred and seventy-five pounds in addition to the sum provided by the Appropriation Ordinance, 1960.

## Section 3

The payment by the Permanent Secretary to the Treasury out of Authority the revenue and other funds of Tanganyika for the several services £* payment specified in the Schedule hereto of the said sum of one million, one hundred and. ninety-nine thousand, seven hundred and seventy-five pounds is hereby declared to have been duly and necessarily paid and is hereby authorized and confirmed.
