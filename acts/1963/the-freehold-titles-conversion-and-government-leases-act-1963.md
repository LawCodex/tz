# The Freehold Titles (Conversion) and Government Leases Act, 1963

THE FREEHOLD TITLES (CONVERSION) AND GOVERNMENT LEASES BILL, 1963 An Act to convert Freehold Estates in land into Leasehold Estates and

\- to provide for the corresponding diminution of other estates and interests in and over land, to declare the incidents of such Leasehold Estates and to provide for the Development of land subject thereto, and to amend the law relating to property in land ENACTED by the Parliament of Tanganyika.
