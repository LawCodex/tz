The Upanga Area (Planning and Development) Ordinance is New section hereby amended by adding, immediately below section 9, the following {> (25283 new section: — “ ‘Residential 9a.—(1) In) this Ordinance ‘residential purposes’ shall, parinclude 22 respect of any land which the Minister may appoint (but hotels, etc. not other land), include—

Provided that no appointment of the Minister under this subsection shall authorize the use of any land contrary to the terms or conditions of the title thereto or to any covenant touching or concerning the land.

(2) An appointment, any amendment thereof, and any conditions imposed under this section shall be deemed to be part of the approved scheme.

(3) In this section, ‘the Minister’ means the Minister responsible for urban local government.”.
