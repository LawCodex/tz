# The Rights of Occupancy (Development Conditions) (Amendment) Act, 1963

NAKALA MTANDAO (ONLINE DOCUMENT) TANGANYIKA No. 47 oF 1963 I ASSENT,

C9..1H SEPTEMBER, 1963

An Act to amend the Rights of Occupancy (Development Conditions) Act, 1963

[20TH.SEPTEMBER, 1963]

Enactep by the. Parliament of Tanganyika.

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Rights of Occupancy (Development Short title and Conditions) (Amendment) Act, 1963, and shall be read as one with Stuction the Rights of Occupancy (Development Conditions) Act, 1963 (herein- Acts; 1963 after called the principal Act). No. 18

## Section 2

Subsection (1) of section 2.of the principal Act-is hereby amended Section 2 as follows: — amended

(a) in the definition “registered”, by inserting immediately after the

word “Ordinance” the words “or under the Registration of Docu-

ments Ordinance”;

(b) by deleting the definition “Registrar” and by substituting therefor

the following new definition:—

“ ‘Registrar’ means, in the case of registered land, the Registrar of Titles appointed under the Land Registration Ordinance. and in the case of land which is not registered land, the

Registrar of Documents appointed under the Registration of Documents Ordinance, and includes any person authorized under those enactments to exercise the powers and functions conferred on such Registrars respectively;”’.

## Section 3

Subsection (3) of section 13 of the principal Act is hereby Section 13 amended by inserting immediately after the word “Ordinance” where of Act it occurs in paragraphs (a) and (b) thereof, the words and comma “or the Registration of Documents Ordinance, as the case may require”.
