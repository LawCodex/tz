## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Metric System (Amendment of Short title Written Laws) Act, 1969.

## Section 2

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires— Interpretation “imperial weights and measures” means imperial weights and measures under and within-the.meaning of the Weights and Measures Ordinance;

“Minister” means the Minister for the time being responsible for legal affairs.

## Section 3

—(1) Where in any written law reference is made to any weight or Amendment measure in terms of any of the imperial weights and measures, the of written Minister may, by Order published in the Gazette, amend the written law as necessary in his opinion to make that reference in terms of the metric weight or measure which is equivalent to that imperial weight or measure.

(2) The Minister may, in any order made by him under this Act—

(a) amend any written law so as to make the reference in question

in terms of the metric weight or measure which, in his opinion, corresponds to the imperial weight or measure in question, and may do so notwithstanding that such corresponding weight or measure is not equivalent to such imperial weight or measure; and

(b) make such supplementary or consequential provisions as are, in

his opinion, necessary or desirable.
