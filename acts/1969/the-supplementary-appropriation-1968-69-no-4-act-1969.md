# The Supplementary Appropriation (1968-69)(No. 4) Act, 1969

NAKALA MTANDAO (ONLINE DOCUMENT)

THE UNITED REPUBLIC, OF TANZANIA

No. 25 OF 1969 I ASSENT,

10 APRIL, 1969

An Act to apply a further sum of seventy-four million, sixty-four thousand, two hundred and five shillings out of the Consolidated Fund to the service of the year ending on the thirtieth day of June, one thousand nine hundred and sixty-nine and to appropriate the supply granted [11th APRIL, 1969] ENACTED by the Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania.

## Section 1

-(l) This Act may be cited as the, Supplementary Appropriation (1968/69) (No. 4) Act 1969, and shall be read, as one with the Appropriation Act, 1968.

(2) The expressions used in this Act shall have the meanings respectively ascribed thereto in the Exchequer and Audit Ordinance.

Short title and construction

## Section 2

The Treasury may issue out of the Consolidated Fund and apply towards the supply granted for the service of the year ending on the thirtieth day of June, one thousand nine hundred and sixty-nine the sum of seventy-four million, sixty-four thousand, two hundred and five shillings.

Issue of Shs. 74,064,205 out of Consolidated Fund Appropriation of moneys voted Appropriation-in-aid

## Section 3

The sum granted by section 2 shall be appropriated to the purposes and in the amounts specified in the second and third columns of the Schedule to this Act.

## Section 4

In addition to the sums granted out of the Consolidated Fund under section 2, there may be applied out of any moneys directed under section 9 of the Exchequer and Audit Ordinance, to be applied as appropriations-in-aid, the amount respectively set forth in the fourth column of the Schedule to this Act.

Cap. 439

## Section 5

(1) The Minister may at any time or times not later than the Power of thirtieth day of June, one thousand nine hundred and sixty-nine the Minister borrow within or outside Tanzania, or partly within and partly outside to borrow Tanzania, any sum or sums not exceeding in the whole seventy-four million, sixty-four thousand, two hundred and five shillings, by way
