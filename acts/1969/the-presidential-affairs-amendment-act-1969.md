# The Presidential Affairs (Amendment) Act, 1969

NAKALA MTANDAO (ONLINE DOCUMENT) THE UNITED. .REPUBLIC OF TANZANIA . - bed No. 31 oF 1969 . > I ASSENT, President 9TH JULY, 1969 An Act to amend the Presidential Affairs Act, 1962 {lira JuLy, 1969]

ENACTED by the Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania.

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Presidential Affairs (Amendment) Short title Act, 1969 and shall be read as one with the Presidential Affairs Act, and construc: 1962. Cap. 502

## Section 2

Section 10 of the Presidential Affairs Act, 1962 is ‘amended by Section 10 of deleting the words ‘‘one hundred thousand pounds” which occur in the Cap. 302 third line thereof and substituting therefor-the words “‘three million shillings’.
