## Section 2

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires— Interpreta- “disposition” means any executory disposition of property and includes a trust in relation to any property;

“Minister” means the Minister for the time being responsible for legal affairs;

“specified disposition’? means any disposition which the Minister may from time to time by order~published in the Gazette specify for the purposes of this Act.

## Section 3

The rule against perpetuities shall not apply to any specified Limitation of disposition, and any such disposition shall not fail by reason only of it application being in contravention of the rule against perpetuities. against
