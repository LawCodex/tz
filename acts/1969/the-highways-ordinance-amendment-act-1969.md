## THIRD SCHEDULE

CONSTITUTION AND PROCEEDINGS OF REGIONAL ROAD BOARDS

## Section 1

In this Schedule “Board”? means a Regional Road Board. Interpreta-

tion

## Section 2

Every Board shall consist. of— Constitution

(a) the Regional Commissioner or his representative; ;

(b) Members of the National, Assembly representing the region for which the Board

is established ;

(c) the Regional Engineer appointed by the Minister ;

(d) the Area Commissioners of the districts in the region;

(e) Executive Officers of all the district councils in the region;

(f) the Regional Agricultural Officer; and

(g) not more than three other members appointed by the Minister after consultation

with the Regional Commissioner.

## Section 3

—(1) The Regional Commissioner shall be the Chairman of the Board. Chairman

(2) The Members of the National Assembly who are members of the Board shall and _Viceelect from amongst themselves a Vice-Chairman. Chairman

(3) The person who has been elected as a Vice-Chairman shall hold office for a period of one year and shall not be eligible for re-election on the the expiry of the term of his office:

Provided that where each of the Members of the National Assembly who is a member of the Board has served as a Vice-Chairman of the Board they may elect any one of their number to be a Vice-Chairman for another term of office.

## Section 4
