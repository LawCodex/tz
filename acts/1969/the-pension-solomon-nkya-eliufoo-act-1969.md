# The Pension (Solomon Nkya Eliufoo) Act, 1969

NAKALA MTANDAO (ONLINE DOCUMENT) THE UNITED REPUBLIC OF TANZANIA on JOY fee face \Q\ Cag No. 36 oF 1969 I ASSENT, —_a Jolurg K Myre PR DU nD onan DD RENE ATES TOW TY ane President 24TH JULy, 1969 An‘Act to award a Pension to Solomon Nkya Eliufoo [25TH Juty, 1969]

ENACTED by the Parliament of the United Republic of Tanzania.

## Section 1

This Act iiay be' cited as the Pensions (SOLOMON Nkya ELIUFOO) Short title Act, 1969.

## Section 2

—(1) There is hereby granted to Sotomon Nxys ELIuFoo during Grant of his lifetime a pension at the rate of twenty-three thousand shillings per Benson fo annum with effect from the first day of September, 1968. Nkya Eliufoo

(2) The provisions of the Pensions Ordinance. shall apply to the Cap. 371 pension granted by subsection (1) as if it was a pension granted under that Ordinance.
